## 1. 心智模型候選 (candidate mental models)

- **Auction Market Theory（拍賣市場理論）**：市場是買賣雙方持續進行的雙向拍賣，目標是發現「公平價值(fair value)」；價格在平衡(balance/range)與失衡(imbalance/breakout)之間循環擺盪。引用：「In reality, the market isn't random. It's organized chaos. It's a constant auction. Buyers and sellers competing to agree on value.」(`i2_zF1A-wI4`)；「on the lower time frames... usually markets range 70 to 80% of the time. And this range is the fear [fair] value」(`i2_zF1A-wI4`)。這是他教學系列的第二支影片（V2 of Beginner Tutorial Series），也是貫穿所有市場更新影片的底層框架，出現在幾乎每支影片（`0Zq5NGUA9E4`, `13ORilePjm0`, `G4qLaNnvmlY`, `prftjeolHTc` 等皆用「consolidation/deviation/acceptance」語言）。

- **Range Extreme to Range Extreme（區間極值到極值的偏移-接受循環）**：價格會從一個區間極值(range low 或 range high)產生偏移(deviation)，再回到區間內被接受(acceptance)，然後移向對側極值；反覆出現，是他核心的操作劇本。引用：「we tend to see deviation outside and then an acceptance back inside the range and then we move from one deviation to the next」(`13ORilePjm0`)；「the current real trading plan is again a theme that we've been going over continuously this bull cycle... we do have ranges. We then tend to find a deviation outside the range」(`13ORilePjm0`)。跨越 `0Zq5NGUA9E4`, `13ORilePjm0`, `IqPP_kKWVtU`, `prftjeolHTc`, `xx-UXzcjH6o` 等多支。

- **SNR Level 記憶與角色反轉（Support/Resistance Flip，"price has a memory"）**：市場「記得」過去的支撐/阻力位；一旦某位階被跌破，回測時常從支撐轉為阻力，反之亦然，這是被困交易者(trapped traders)平倉造成。引用：「price has a memory. When we return to the level, it's potential that we may see a flip from resistance into support.」(`13ORilePjm0`)；在教學影片中系統化陳述：「A level that was strong support in the past can once broken turn into a future resistance... This phenomenon is called row [role] reversal or support and resistance flip」(`Fz0Ha11xTlY`)。

- **Volume Profile 概念群（Point of Control / Naked POC / Value Area High-Low / Low Volume Node）**：用歷史成交量分布位置作為價格磁鐵與支撐阻力參考，尤其「naked point of control」（未被回測過的高量單）被視為價格磁鐵。引用：「You have this naked point control which tends to act as a magnet for price. Price is drawn into it」(`13ORilePjm0`)。幾乎所有市場更新影片都使用（`0Zq5NGUA9E4`, `G4qLaNnvmlY`, `gZjxgPACaME`, `x5f-vpR6lfc` 等）。

- **Volume 作為「測謊機」：Correction vs Reversal**：價格逆勢移動但成交量萎縮 = 修正(correction)，趨勢只是休息未結束；價格逆勢移動且成交量放大（尤其伴隨關鍵位跌破）= 真反轉。引用：「A move without volume backing is a correction. A move with volume backing is a reversal or continuation.」「volume is our lie detector. Every time price does something dramatic, check the volume.」(`7bPirCNhxNA`)。並用 2017 年 BTC 頂部（近 19,700-20k，頂部量能低於平均）作為經典案例佐證。

- **Confluence Stacking（合流變數堆疊）**：多個獨立變數（SNR位、POC、費波納契0.618、心理整數、供需區）疊加在同一價位 = 更高機率的反應區。引用：「essentially just multiple levels or variables stacked on top of each other which gives potentially a higher probability of the level holding」(`13ORilePjm0`)；「there's a lot of confluence in this region... a lot of confluence in that area to look for short」(`x5f-vpR6lfc`)。此為他選擇進場點的核心篩選機制，貫穿幾乎全部影片。

- **Trapped Traders / SFP（Swing Failure Pattern，流動性掃蕩後反轉）**：價格短暫刺穿某高/低點掃蕩流動性，誘捕晚進場的多/空單，然後反向運行。引用：「we had this really nice SFP, dropped down」(多次)；教學中明確定義：「the trapped trader phenomenon... those trap sellers will rush to buy back to cover their shorts near break even」(`Fz0Ha11xTlY`)。

- **Trading vs Investing 二元劃分**：短線交易（依賴槓桿/lower time frame，波段操作）與長線投資（spot 現貨持有）是完全不同的決策邏輯，不應混淆；即使短線看空，長線仍可看多。引用：「there is a difference between trading and investing」(`0Zq5NGUA9E4`)；「trading and investing are two different things. And I do think sometimes people misconceptualize what I'm discussing」(`G4qLaNnvmlY`)。反覆出現（`0Zq5NGUA9E4`, `G4qLaNnvmlY`, `IqPP_kKWVtU` 隱含）。

- **BTC 是王（"BTC is the king"）——大餅主導性**：alts 只是 BTC 走勢的延伸/放大器，必須先看 BTC 再看 altcoin。引用：「you need to make sure that you're always paying attention to BTC because BTC is the king and if BTC goes down, everything goes down.」(`20caZbfa5uk`)。

- **概率式、去情緒化決策（percentage guesses, not certainties）**：所有分析都是機率性猜測，不是預言；交易不應帶情緒。引用：「All we can do is look at the levels and look at the fractal patterns... use that to make educational guesses, which is all ever this is. They're all percentage guesses because no one can say for certain」(`prftjeolHTc`)；「it's not beneficial to be emotional」(`0Zq5NGUA9E4`)。

- **If-Then 條件式思考（非盲目下單，"coder mentality"）**：不是在關鍵位盲目掛單，而是「如果到了這個位置，且發生了某個確認事件，那麼才進場」的條件式邏輯。引用：「it's not just binary, it's more of if then. So, if we get here, and then something happens, well, then maybe I'll look to get into a trade in that region.」(`x5f-vpR6lfc`)；「there is kind of an almost coder mentality to it」(`x5f-vpR6lfc`)。

- **Staircase Up, Elevator Down**：上漲通常是緩慢、低動能的階梯式爬升（伴隨量能不足的隱憂），下跌則急速劇烈。引用：「we kind of use the term staircase up, elevator down, which is probably one of the more concerning things about the current price action」(`GmvgCClOeEg`)。

- **市場循環階段論（Accumulation → Bull → Distribution → Bear，及與 ICT「Power of Three」的自覺對照）**：他承認自己描述的「consolidation-manipulation/deviation-expansion」與 ICT 的 Power of Three 概念相似，但持續使用自己「auction market theory」的詞彙。引用：「A very familiar setup that a lot of you may know is power of three where you have accumulation manipulation and then an expansion phase. So, I've seen that referenced multiple times... I've referred to them as auction market theory」(`eXAgteOal4Q`)。（推斷：他刻意保留自己的詞彙體系，暗示他知道自己的框架與 ICT 系派有交集但不願直接採用 ICT 術語。）

## 2. 決策啟發式 (decision heuristics)

- 若歷史新高/新低出現但成交量未同步放大（低於均量），則不信任該突破的持續性，視為缺乏機構參與的假動作（2017 頂部案例）。來源(`7bPirCNhxNA`)。
- 若價格以放大成交量跌破/突破關鍵支撐/阻力，則「必須順勢而為，不可逆勢對做」；若後續再破一個支撐位且仍帶量，可考慮加碼同方向部位。來源(`7bPirCNhxNA`)。
- 不要在關鍵位「盲目掛單(blind bidding)」；需等待額外確認（價格行為反應、成交量、或 Exocharts 等訂單流工具顯示的興趣）才進場。來源(`0Zq5NGUA9E4`, `x5f-vpR6lfc`)。
- 若想做空，最佳進場點是在價格「回抽」到你的關鍵阻力位時進場，而非追空；做多同理，等回踩支撐再進場，而非追高。來源(`qzVXHfFZ644`)：「you want to get your entries when price is doing the opposite thing than what you're trying to enter in as.」
- 進場前先設想「若這個位置未能守住會發生什麼」——同時規劃兩種劇本（守住 vs 跌破）。來源(`Fz0Ha11xTlY`)。
- 若跌破某一核心結構性位階（例如 2025 yearly open 或某週期關鍵區），必須重新評估整個週期偏空/偏多論述(bias)，不能死守原有劇本。來源(`G4qLaNnvmlY`)：「you then have to reassess the whole kind of bias」。
- 心理整數關卡（round numbers，如 100k、120k）傾向於被「搶先跑(front run)」，也因群眾心理下單而形成真實支撐/阻力。來源(`0Zq5NGUA9E4`, `G4qLaNnvmlY`)。
- 對於交易部位（非現貨長期持倉），使用小額風險（1%、0.5%、0.25% 帳戶風險）；現貨與交易部位邏輯分開管理。來源(`GPm6WmHSy9Q`)。
- 若價格劇烈、單邊地（"very aggressive move"）衝向某關鍵位，不宜在該次衝擊直接盲目進場反向單，須等待動能減弱訊號（如 RSI 動能鈍化、緩慢爬升後停滯）。來源(`G4qLaNnvmlY`)。
- DXY（美元指數）與風險資產反向連動：DXY 走弱通常利多 BTC/加密資產；DXY 走強突破關鍵位則需提高警覺，可能引發熊市。來源(`G4qLaNnvmlY`, `IqPP_kKWVtU`, `c7JNXldHsPc`)。
- 週末/低成交量時段（如美股休市的週末）不應過度分析低時間框架雜訊，因為流動性稀薄。來源(`c7JNXldHsPc`)。
- 交易區間(range)比追逐大波段(impulse legs)更容易操作且更寬容；多數人試圖抓大波段反而虧損更多，應學會在區間極值附近交易。來源(`qzVXHfFZ644`)。

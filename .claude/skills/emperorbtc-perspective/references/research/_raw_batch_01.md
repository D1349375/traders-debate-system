## 1. 心智模型候選 (candidate mental models)

- **拍賣市場理論 (Auction Market Theory) — 擴張/整理循環**：市場在「擴張(expansion)—整理(consolidation)」之間循環，價格不斷尋求公允價值(fair value)。引用：「if you've watched our auction market theory where price is constantly seeking fair value and you have these long consolidations after an expansive move. So expansion consolidation expansion consolidation」(`1EdQGLR6SA0`)；「market conditions vary from expansive moves and into consolidations. And you tend to have an expansive move followed by a consolidation. And that is simply referred to as a auction market theory」(`7Dn6R2QDAjU`)；「auction market theory if we were to look at it from that perspective where you have consolidation break up market seeks fair value」(`UJGfKNtc5wI`)。出現於幾乎每支影片，是他最核心、反覆使用的框架標籤。

- **Range 交易 / 從一個極端到另一個極端 (range extreme to range extreme / deviation to deviation)**：市場在區間高低點之間移動，交易的高機率動作發生在區間邊緣（extremities），而非區間中點。引用：「when trading a range, what you really want to be doing is trading the extremities of them range」(`7Dn6R2QDAjU`)；「price moving from range extreme to range extreme」(`nDBjsAZq2s8`)；「we've moved from this range low to range high and then maybe back to the lows」(`UJGfKNtc5wI`)；「Anything in between is is low probability in my opinion」(`7Dn6R2QDAjU`)。跨幾乎所有市場更新影片反覆出現，屬於他最常用的分析骨架。

- **Key S&R Zone（關鍵支撐/阻力區）+ 角色反轉 (support-resistance flip)**：一個層級可能因為多次被觸及（resistance, resistance, resistance...）而被反覆標記，一旦跌破/突破就會反轉角色（resistance→support 或反之）。引用：「resistance, resistance, resistance, resistance, resistance. And now we've come back down and we found support within it」(`1EdQGLR6SA0`)；「we've kind of flipped the range lows」(`1EdQGLR6SA0`)；「flip this key SNR zone bullish」多次出現(`nDBjsAZq2s8`, `FE7yLUDXvaQ`)。

- **成交量分佈 / Volume Profile 核心概念（POC、naked POC、value area high/low、volume imbalance）**：用固定區間成交量分佈(fixed range volume profile)找出 point of control（成交量最大處）、naked/negative point of control（未被回測過的POC，具磁吸效應）、value area high/low、以及 volume imbalance（單邊成交造成的價格缺口，日後傾向被「填補」而非提供支撐/阻力）。引用：「naked point of control which may act as a draw for price」(`BteRIzKmCnE`)；「volume imbalances tend to be more of a filling in procedure rather than a potential reversal area」(`7Dn6R2QDAjU`)；「the naked point of controls here acting as a reversal point」(`nDBjsAZq2s8`)。跨幾乎每支影片使用，是他相對於純 price-action 派的差異化工具。

- **流動性 (liquidity) 掃蕩 / SFP (swing failure pattern)**：等高/等低點（relatively equal highs/lows）代表流動性堆積處，價格傾向去「掃」這些點位（sweep/raid），常以 SFP（假突破後反轉）形式出現，是他判斷反轉的核心訊號。引用：「relatively equal highs...become areas to target as pockets of liquidity」(`7Dn6R2QDAjU`)；「we've had this SFP down here...a nice SFP down here」(`FE7yLUDXvaQ`)；「if you have a lot of highs that are untagged...when you get an inverse move on the other side, they tend to be quite aggressive cuz they can just slice through all the liquidity resting above the previous highs」(`fRERo_CM3Ks`)。

- **順勢優先於逆勢 (trend/momentum over counter-trend fading, "don't fight the tide")**：即使某個逆勢設置的所有變數看起來都吻合，只要與高時間框架趨勢方向相反，就是低機率交易；順著大方向即使細節不完美也更容易獲利。引用：「even if you're wrong on the actual trade variables, you still have a high probability chance of being essentially lucky and just catching the next move down rather than having the perfect long setup...because the higher time frame trend is against you」(`7Dn6R2QDAjU`)；「the best thing to do when trading is to try your best to be with the high tide」(`njht8VBN0hk`)；「In momentum markets, it's better to chase with conviction than to wait and miss the move entirely. Best entries often come when you trust momentum, not just textbook sells」(`nDBjsAZq2s8`)。

- **Premium / Discount（溢價區/折價區）**：以區間中點(0.5 / mid-range point)劃分，中點以上為 premium（不找多），中點以下為 discount（不找空）。引用：「anything above the 0.5 is the premium of the range and anything sub 0.5 is discount...as price is entering a discounted range you really don't want to be looking for shorts...when price is entering a premium you don't necessarily want to be looking for longs」(`7Dn6R2QDAjU`)。

- **市場結構 (market structure) — higher high/low, lower high/low, break of structure**：用高低點序列判斷趨勢方向與轉折（"shift in structure"/"shift in momentum"）。引用：「we have high, low, then we pushed beyond creating this as the higher low...Now we are looking to see if potentially we are putting in a lower low」(`4yB2IjsuqLI`)；「we had this shift in structure which tells us that buyers are potentially losing momentum and sellers are potentially entering the market」(`SrKw5FZuaXs`)。

- **市場是分形的 (fractal nature of price)**：同樣的型態會在任何時間框架重複出現（月線到1分鐘線），只是高時間框架更重要。引用：「It's really important to know the price is fractal by nature. The same patterns that form on a monthly time frame can form on a 1 minute time frame. And although on the monthly time frame it carries more significance, the nature is still that the same logic can apply」(`nDBjsAZq2s8`)。

- **RSI 是動能指標，不是超買超賣買賣訊號**：RSI 衡量動能強弱而非「價格是否過高/過低」；強趨勢中 RSI 可長期停留在 70 以上或 30 以下；背離只在關鍵位置（range high/low, VWAP, resistance）才有意義，且只是確認/警訊而非入場觸發。引用：「overbought does not equal time to sell and oversold does not equal time to buy...The RSI doesn't predict the price. It tells you how strong price already is. Trade with that information, not against it」(`TOVG__EBmxs`)。他還提出「RSI 50 level trend strategy」作為具體的順勢策略。

## 2. 決策啟發式 (decision heuristics)

- 若價格處在整理區間內，且尚未對關鍵位有反應(no reaction)，則傾向於該關鍵位最終會「破位」而非撐住："a nice rule of thumb is that if price is consolidating in around the key level and it hasn't actually got a reaction from it, it tends to lead to a breakdown"(`BRTqQEvWEcM`)。
- 若某個高點/低點在多次測試後被「假突破再反轉」(SFP)掃過，代表可能是反轉點；若連續多次測試同一方向都失敗（如四次觸及同一高點：three drives 應為三次但這裡出現四次刺探），要等第四次伴隨方向性成交量放量確認再進場，而非在前幾次就搶進： "early shorts punished here massively until we get this high...you need to have patience for the market to at least reveal its intended direction"(`7Dn6R2QDAjU`)。
- 若在區間交易（ranging market），偏空/偏多的高機率交易方向應根據高時間框架趨勢決定，而非只根據區間內位置："you would look for primarily shorts rather than longs because you believe shorts have more potential to run"(`4yB2IjsuqLI`)。
- 若某等高/等低點群集長期未被觸及(untagged)，則反向的移動一旦發生會非常劇烈，因為會「一路切穿」堆積的流動性：見上方 fractal liquidity 心智模型(`fRERo_CM3Ks`)。
- 若成交量在下跌/上漲中出現（volume confirming the move），則突破有效；若無方向性成交量，不視為突破確認："we get our above medium or above average volume above our median on the average line and increase letting us know this move is confirmed"(`7Dn6R2QDAjU`)。
- 交易區间時，優先在極端位置(extremities)交易，避免在區間中段(equilibrium/mid-range)交易，除非是用作確認位: "trading the mid-range point can be beneficial or at least acknowledging where it is"(`7Dn6R2QDAjU`)——是次要用法，非首選進場點。
- 若倉位的失效點(invalidation)被觸及，即接受錯誤並離場，不硬凹："sometimes it's nice to accept you're wrong in your invalidation, then wait for it to maybe end up in around here where you're kind of forced to not hold the trade any longer and then you lose more than you anticipated. So sometimes your best to cut your losses"(`njht8VBN0hk`)。
- 不需要每筆交易都獲利；用風險報酬比與勝率的組合思考系統獲利能力："You can have a 2hour fixed return rate and a 40% win rate or something along them lines and still be wildly profitable. You don't have to win every trade"(`7Dn6R2QDAjU`)。
- 交易 altcoin 時，先用基本面(fundamentals)篩選要交易哪個幣，再用技術分析決定進出場時機，兩者缺一不可："fundamentals analysis paired with technical analysis is very important when trading all coins...that selection needs to be followed by fundamental analysis first, then using technical analysis to find your entries"(`njht8VBN0hk`)。
- 不對非 BTC 的 alt（尤其是 meme/低基本面幣）做深入技術分析，只做 level-to-level 的機會型交易："Very difficult to do technical analysis on coins like this...playing this level to level"(`njht8VBN0hk`, 談 pump)。
- 交易 altcoin 前必先確認 BTC 的方向，因為 alt 走勢高度依賴 BTC："it's really important to know what BTC is doing first and foremost"(`62MJPdzPBA8`)；"similar to eat it's all BTC dependent"(`Mu1IOILtYPk`)。
- 若已在牛市中錯過低點（pico bottom），不要期待「完美的區間+需求區回測」會再出現；在強趨勢中這種理想設置往往不會再現，應接受用不完美的方式跟上動能："if you miss the pico bottom, don't expect a clean range plus demand retest. Those ideal setups often never come in strong trends"(`nDBjsAZq2s8`)。
- 不要因為自己有既定方向偏見（bias）就無視市場正在告訴你的訊息（例如不斷把支撐位往上移來自我安慰）："What they start to do is they move their support zones higher...it becomes a form of self soyop"(`nDBjsAZq2s8`)。
- 現貨部位大、已獲利頗豐時，不需要過度交易（overtrade），可以讓子彈飛，保持耐心："as a major BTC spot holder, I don't really overtrade because I can just make significant profits in my spot positions"(`nDBjsAZq2s8`)。
- 使用 Coinbase 溢價(Coinbase premium)作為動能訊號：若 Coinbase 現貨價格對比其他交易所（如 Bybit 永續合約）持續有溢價，往往是買進訊號，可讓動能交易者提前進場而不必等待傳統價格行為確認："there's a consistent premium on Coinbase versus other exchanges and it's often a good buy signal. So momentum traders can use this as a signal to get long quickly before the rest of the market catches up"(`nDBjsAZq2s8`)。
- 機構/國庫（如MicroStrategy、Metaplanet）買盤是「強迫買入」，資金到位後必須立刻買進，不是慢慢等特定技術位置，因此這類買盤會造成單邊急拉而非緩漲："These buyers are not retail. They move the market and when they receive capital from investors, they are forced to buy immediately"(`nDBjsAZq2s8`)。

## 5. 矛盾 / 立場演變 (contradictions & evolution)

- **對「加密市場是否已成熟、不再劇烈崩盤」的立場前後矛盾**：在 `4yB2IjsuqLI`（推測為較早，內容提及"first video of the year"、"merry Christmas and happy new year"）中他說「I think the market has changed.

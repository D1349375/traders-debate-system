## 1. 心智模型候選 (candidate mental models)

- **拍賣市場理論 / 價格尋找公允價值 (Auction Market Theory / price seeks fair value)**：市場在「整理(consolidation/range)→衝量(impulse/expansion)→整理」之間循環；不會永遠漲、也不會直接歸零，更不會鋸齒狀反覆震盪。引用：「markets move from consolidation or... a range into an impulse or an expansion... after an expansive move, price will naturally seek to consolidate in that area before the next impulse leg」(`ZJF4JQ8LkNg`)；「anyone that has watched my market auction theory video and how price always seeks fair value」(`VBhlHl-tIGo`)。跨檔：`ZJF4JQ8LkNg`, `VBhlHl-tIGo`, `zDkpOvgjlck`, `AEZ13WIGPTo` 均反覆出現此框架，屬核心心智模型。

- **Range 兩端理論 (range extreme to range extreme / deviation-to-deviation)**：價格會從一個 range 極端擺盪到另一個極端；先偏離(deviate)出 range 再被拉回，是反覆出現的碎形(fractal)結構。引用：「we will move from one range extreme to another」(`ZJF4JQ8LkNg`)；「we've gone from range extreme, mid-range point acted as resistance, and then we have deviated below range lows」(`cvhPNpZWJks`)；「a fractal price pattern where you have these range highs... then you get deviation outside of the range」(`AEZ13WIGPTo`)。出現於 `8DjZKAIghNE`, `AEZ13WIGPTo`, `cvhPNpZWJks`, `mZ1HxAYXXhc`, `xhLngR4la74` 等近乎每支影片。

- **成交量剖面 / Point of Control 磁吸理論 (Volume Profile / POC as magnet)**：成交量剖面中的 POC（尤其「naked/negative point of control」，即之後未被重新測試的 POC）會像磁鐵一樣吸引價格回訪。引用：「The point of control acts as a magnet for price. Markets tend to return to it... This is often why we see naked point of controls being such strong draws for liquidity」(`n240i2LFNwc`)；「there is a naked point of control just overhead. So you may see price reach there as they do tend to be a magnet for price」(`VBhlHl-tIGo`)。跨檔：`8DjZKAIghNE`, `AEZ13WIGPTo`, `g6VHpOMCkPo`, `kmEfAFWwDf8`, `mZ1HxAYXXhc`。

- **關鍵 S&R 區/信心疊加 (Key S&R zone / confluence stacking)**：一個區域若同時具備多個獨立訊號（前高前低、POC、心理整數關卡、費波那契、成交量缺口）疊加，才是高機率交易區；單一訊號不足採信。引用：「This area for me is super clean... previous Range High plus the weekly demand Zone」「we now have a daily level, a fib level, and a volume node all stacking up between 79K and 75K. This is huge confluence」(`kuadEmMi2nc`)；「so you have the SFP of the low, you have the naked point of control, and you also have the 100k key psychological level. So this was a huge confluence zone」(`mZ1HxAYXXhc`)。

- **成交量 x 趨勢 四象限矩陣 (four volume-price scenarios)**：這是他自建的核心分析工具。引用：「Trend up, volume up, equals conviction... Trend up, volume down, no conviction... Trend down, volume up, is conviction... Trend down, volume down, no conviction」(`n240i2LFNwc`)。強調「這是觀察不是鐵律」：「The four volume price scenarios are observations, built on patterns that play out frequently, not laws of physics」(`n240i2LFNwc`)。

- **高時間框架決定方向/低時間框架找進場點 (top-down / higher timeframe dictates bias)**：先在高時間框架標記關鍵水平，避免掉入低時間框架雜訊，等待價格進入高時間框架關鍵區才在低時間框架尋找確認進場。引用：「what you start off with is a higher time frame level... this helps us to avoid all of the lower time frame insignificant levels」(`Jv5FpmwD5KA`)；「it makes more sense to be on the higher time frames side of order flow because... you're in a nice directional bias」(`8sa9RFOdo2E`)。

- **SFP / 流動性掃蕩導致陷阱 (SFP / liquidity sweep traps traders, fuels momentum)**：價格短暫刺穿前高/前低（stop hunt），使一批交易者被套牢或被迫平倉，進而驅動反向或延續的衝量。引用：「when you get these trap longers in here... this was the telltale sign」(`KXQIt0rIjlE`)；「SFP with the SFP... naked point of control just so 100k」(`mZ1HxAYXXhc`)。他明白表示這與 ICT 用語「liquidity sweep」是同一件事，但傾向用自己的詞彙：「SFP or liquidity sweep, whichever terminology you would prefer to subscribe to」(`8DjZKAIghNE`) — 顯示他有意識地與 ICT 用語做區隔但概念部分重疊。

- **BTC 是王者 (BTC is the king)**：分析任何 altcoin 前必須先看 BTC，altcoin 表現高度依附 BTC 走勢與「資金輪動」(BTC 先漲→大盤/中盤/小盤依序補漲)。引用：「BTC is the king. As always, before you look at your alt, sometimes you need to look where BTC is」(`8sa9RFOdo2E`)；「everything will follow BTC one way or another」(`ZJF4JQ8LkNg`)。

- **智慧錢/機構足跡追蹤 (smart money footprint tracking, e.g. Bitfinex longs)**：透過非典型的鏈上/交易所倉位數據（如 Bitfinex BTC/USD 多單）觀察機構式「逢弱買進、逢強賣出」的持倉行為，作為輔助信號而非交易訊號本身。引用：「they build positionally... they're buying into weakness and selling into strength... while everyone else is panicking about chop, smart money is doing the same thing they always do. Quietly buying when it's uncomfortable」(`kuadEmMi2nc`)。

- **碎形本質 (price is fractal)**：同樣的型態在週線、日線、4小時、1小時、1分鐘都會重演，高時間框架型態權重更高。引用：「price is fractal by nature. The same patterns that unfold on a weekly time frame, or daily time frame, also appear on the 4-hour, 1-hour, all the way down to the 1-minute time frame. Of course, higher time frame patterns hold more significant weight」(`xhLngR4la74`)。

- **敘事/地緣政治不改變長期趨勢 (macro narrative ≠ long-term trend changer)**：戰爭、地緣衝突等新聞造成短期反應但不改變既有結構性趨勢（除非是流動性直接被抽走的事件如 FTX/Luna）。引用：「the reality of it is Bitcoin was on a decline anyway and it didn't really affect the long term scenario. I mean FTX did... because that was a direct threat and liquidity extraction」(`zDkpOvgjlck`)。

## 2. 決策啟發式 (decision heuristics)

- 若高時間框架趨勢與低時間框架逆勢訊號衝突，優先尊重高時間框架方向；逆勢 setup 「不是完美的 setup」。來源(`8sa9RFOdo2E`)：「if you have higher time frame bullish structure and you have the perfect short setup on the lower time frame, it is not a perfect setup.」
- Range 高點傾向做空/賣、range 低點傾向做多/買，重複執行直到失效為止，不必自作聰明過度解讀。來源(`8DjZKAIghNE`)：「at range highs, it tends to be beneficial to be a seller. And then at range lows, it's beneficial to be a buyer. You kind of just rinse and repeat this until it doesn't work rather than being a genius about it.」
- 若處於盤整/中點/equilibrium 且缺乏明確結構確認，判定為低機率環境，選擇不交易優於亂猜方向。來源(`8DjZKAIghNE`)：「it is equally as important to know when not to trade as opposed to knowing when to trade... I believe that the current market conditions are low probability.」同樣見於 `mZ1HxAYXXhc`：「Midrange points, in my opinion, without the correct market structure, are very low probability because they could go either way.」
- 戰爭/危機新聞爆出、群眾恐慌賣出時 = 買進訊號；停戰/樂觀消息、群眾追高買進時 = 考慮賣出訊號（反群眾）。來源(`8DjZKAIghNE`)：「once you start hearing talks of World War III and the selling starts, it's a good time to buy. And then, when you start hearing news that the war isn't going to happen and the market start to pump, it's a good time to sell.」並在 `mZ1HxAYXXhc` 中回顧驗證此規則生效。
- 99% 交易者虧損，因此不要跟隨群眾/大眾情緒。來源(`8DjZKAIghNE`)：「99% of people lose trading, so it's best to not follow the masses.」
- 進場前必須等待「成交量放大 + 結構確認」（例如突破後回測、swing high 突破），不要僅憑單一 volume spike 衝動進場。來源(`n240i2LFNwc`)：「A volume spike gets your attention. Structure gives you your entry... The best entries come slightly later than the initial spike, and patience is part of your edge.」
- 若成交量僅略高於均值，應縮小倉位；若成交量顯著高於均值且結構吻合，可放大信心與倉位。來源(`n240i2LFNwc`)：「When volume is significantly above average and your setup aligns, that's a higher conviction trade. When volume is only marginally above average, size down.」
- 交易量能分析必須同時檢視「比自己交易時間框架更高一階」的時間框架成交量，高時間框架的量能訊號權重更高。來源(`n240i2LFNwc`)：「check volume on the time frame above yours as well. A high volume move on the daily chart carries more weight than a high volume move on the 5-minute chart.」
- Scalp/短線交易達成 TP1 後，將停損移至保本，避免獲利單轉虧損單。來源(`ZJF4JQ8LkNg`)：「when you take your TP1, you can in reality move stops to a more conservative break even because you don't want to have a winning position become lose position.」
- 進場後不要過度管理部位，讓行情資訊自然展開再依計畫調整。來源(`ZJF4JQ8LkNg`)：「you don't necessarily have to overmanage your trades. That's kind of sometimes the worst thing you can do.」
- 若持有較高時間框架偏空/偏多的既定立場（例如熊市 PTSD），需依新資訊調整，不可硬套舊有情緒化偏見。來源(`xhLngR4la74`)：「I don't hold biases too strongly. The overall trend is bearish, but as new information is made available to you, you must adapt. Otherwise, in a bull market, if you have bear market PTSD, you will try and short everything.」
- 對於順勢方向（如高時間框架多頭）的交易目標可以較樂觀；逆勢方向（空單）的目標則需保守，不預期大幅擴張。來源(`Jv5FpmwD5KA`)：「when you are shortening be conservative with your targets. Don't expect expansion to the lower side of the market... if you are entering longs, you can be a little bit more optimistic.」
- 牛市賺到「改變人生的錢」時，務必分批獲利了結、支付自己，而非只截圖 P&L；避免情緒化交易（貪婪與恐懼皆同樣有害）。來源(`ZJF4JQ8LkNg`)：「If you're making life-changing money, don't just screenshot your P&L. Try and take some profits along the way... please avoid emotional trading, good or bad. It never does anything good to your trading.」
- Altcoin 只在深度折價/高價值區間才考慮買進，不要「嫁」給倉位；創辦人/大戶會沿途賣出，不應期待他們撐盤。來源(`y2fnBTRkCPw`)：「absolutely do not marry these bags because the founders will absolutely be selling the token on the way up.」
- 現貨長線倉位應避免使用槓桿，因為心理上更能承受帳面虧損並持續向下加碼攤平成本。來源(`xhLngR4la74`)：「that's why it's best to avoid using leverage, especially. Then, you can always purchase more in these regions and that will bring your average cost down.」

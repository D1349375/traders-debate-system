## 1. 心智模型候選 (candidate mental models)

- **Risk Metric（0-1 風險帶）**：自建量化指標，把任何資產（BTC/ETH/XRP/金/銀）的價格歷史壓縮成 0-1 的風險分數，用來判斷「目前是便宜還是貴」，而非預測明天漲跌。引用：「if you look at the risk metric for xrp it's currently its current risk is 871」、「it's only spent 80 Days in the 08 to 0.9 wristban... only spent 35 days in the 0.9 to1 wristban」(`JsBssA7ovCE`)。同一套風險帶邏輯也用在 gold（「the $3,000 level was a pretty important level because that corresponded to around the .8 risk level」, `LLOL3Ku4Tpc`）。跨多支影片反覆出現，是他最核心、最獨有的分析工具。

- **對數迴歸「公允價值」帶 (fair value logarithmic regression trend line)**：對整個加密貨幣總市值擬合一條單調遞增的對數迴歸線，視為「公允價值」，資產類別會週期性地低估/高估，並圍繞這條線震盪。這是「Beauty of Mathematics」系列每月固定更新的核心圖表。引用：「the fair value logarithmic aggression trend line is at around 3.725 trillion. This represents an undervaluation of approximately 19%」(`4byEiDYDOKQ`)；「I do think the asset class will go to 10 trillion plus or minus a few trillion. And as we go to sleep at night, we cannot help but wonder what's a few trillion dollars among friends」(在幾乎每支 Beauty of Mathematics 影片結尾重複，見 `4byEiDYDOKQ`, `E6xmZR6HRGw`, `UwH6OKKypDo`, `V3DPOp7SbTE`, `jxDDnGc1emI`, `vWLAcGD3AJc`)。

- **Bull Market Support Band / Bear Market Resistance Band（同一條帶，隨週期身份互換）**：20週SMA + 21週EMA組合，牛市時是支撐，熊市/中期年時角色反轉變成壓力。金價則用月線版本（20月SMA+21月EMA）。引用：「in mid-term years Bitcoin tends to find resistance at this band... it's no longer the bull market support band」(`1E2cJu2ZjEs`)；「until proven otherwise, the bull market support band is the bare market resistance band」(`BhI9mS770yA`)；金價版本見 `sjt1LV2iDog`。

- **四年週期／「ROI from the low」跨週期疊圖**：把每個週期從低點起算的天數 vs. ROI 疊在一起比較，用來驗證「頂/底是否準時發生」，而非用敘事。引用：「this cycle lasted, it topped on day 1,062... The cycle before this one topped on day 1,059... The cycle before that one topped on day 1,068」(`CJCyxfuo73o`)。他反覆用這張圖反駁「四年週期已死」的說法，出現在 `-m-2wAhiB1k`, `CJCyxfuo73o`, `slTIiS-Y65k`, `zrb9eDmmaMg` 等多支影片。

- **山寨幣是「頂多是震盪器」(altcoins are oscillators at best)／比特幣主導率 (Bitcoin dominance) 框架**：山寨幣兌 BTC 長期趨勢是下跌（無論 BTC 本身漲跌），資金會週期性回流到 BTC/ETH（「blue chip dominance」）。用 Satoshi（BTC計價）而非美元衡量投資組合表現。引用：「altcoins are oscillators at best」(多次出現，如 `EnnKjDHiqyI`, `FRLK6NvcjTk`, `UEatlG8csiA`)；「the Satoshi valuation of your crypto portfolio is a lot more important than the USD valuation」(`3gObVL_2eL8`)；「all Bitcoin pairs will go to 0.25. No true sustained alt season has ever happened until that event」(`EnnKjDHiqyI`)。

- **商業週期圖 (ITC Business Cycle Chart)**：S&P500 除以（失業率²×聯邦基金利率×通膨率），再除以 M2 貨幣供給，用來判斷處於商業週期的哪個階段（早/中/晚期），並主張「唯有透過衰退才能重置到低點」。引用：「S&P 500 divided by the unemployment rate squared, multiply it by inflation... and multiply that by interest rates... every time this metric basically goes off of this level down here, we eventually return to it... the way in which we eventually get it down there, historically, is a recession」(`FiztJuyl7p4`)；也大量用於 `boZbiynTEQ0`, `w1dJjaF1g9g`。

- **敘事跟隨價格，而非相反 (narrative follows price, not the other way around)**：明確的認識論立場——市場先動，敘事才被發明來解釋它，因此不該用敘事去預測。引用：「narrative follows price not the other way around」(`boZbiynTEQ0`, `il089bNt-4g` 等多次)；「Bitcoin normally corrects at January in January of post having years... so why do we need to go find a narrative to support that outcome because we already knew that was a likely outcome」(`boZbiynTEQ0`)。

- **季節性「弱勢窗口」(windows of weakness) 與月度報酬模式**：反覆比對歷史同期（2月低、3月高、4-6月弱、9月拉回、10月反彈、Post-halving年Q4見頂）等固定時間模式，作為預測的骨幹。引用：「windows of weakness are early February, early April. Then June. If not June, the next one's October」(`6RBevGGz1Pw`)；「Bitcoin is green in both July and August, right?... September was red」(`EnnKjDHiqyI`, `G5IEOvGueGU`, `il089bNt-4g`)。

- **死亡交叉反彈模式 (death cross rally)**：不理會死亡交叉的字面凶兆，而是統計上「死亡交叉當天/附近幾乎必成局部低點，隨後反彈」，是機械式規律而非傳統技術分析詮釋。引用：「Bitcoin dumps just before the death cross... history shows you should not [panic sell] on the day of the death cross because that ends up marking a local low」(`DeFbx6cCHro`)。

- **50週均線作為週期終結的機械判準**：連續兩根週線收在50週SMA之下 = 牛市週期結束的確認訊號；之後依序測試50→100→200週均線。引用：「on a couple of weekly closes below the 50week moving average, that would be a sign that the cycle would be over」(`VRjQEBmNS0E`)；「every time Bitcoin crossed below the 50-week moving average, it was always confirmation that the bull market was over」(`6RBevGGz1Pw`)。

## 2. 決策啟發式 (decision heuristics)

- 若風險指標達到 0.9-1.0 高風險帶，歷史上該資產在此區間停留時間很短（幾天到最多兩三週），則預期即將進入盤整/回調，即使長期仍可能更高。來源(`JsBssA7ovCE`)。
- 若 total3(或total2)-USDT 除以 BTC 跌到約 0.25（含穩定幣約 0.25，不含約 0.29），視為山寨幣對 BTC 的「歷史投降/區間低點」訊號，是真正 alt season 出現前的必要條件。來源(`EnnKjDHiqyI`, `NREjLugm83U`)。
- 若 Bitcoin 連續兩根週線收盤低於50週SMA，判定牛市週期結束，之後預期50-70%的高點回撤。來源(`VRjQEBmNS0E`, `6RBevGGz1Pw`)。
- 若初請失業金 (initial claims) 低於30萬，判定「非衰退區域」；一旦持續印出30萬以上，開始擔心負向循環（裁員→找不到工作→消費下降→更多裁員）。來源(`3gObVL_2eL8`, `BT716hsXI_Y`, `boZbiynTEQ0`)。
- 若整體加密貨幣市值總量長期低於/貼近「公允價值」迴歸線，判定比特幣主導率會持續上升；唯有市值「持久地」突破公允價值線之上，山寨幣才會真正加入行情。來源(`4byEiDYDOKQ`, `V3DPOp7SbTE`)。
- 若 ETH/USD 觸及其對數迴歸下軌（「go home」），視為 ETH/BTC 觸底的必要條件之一；但若 Bitcoin USD 繼續下跌，ETH/BTC 仍可能繼續破底。來源(`FRLK6NvcjTk`)。
- 不嘗試精確地做空/擇時熊市中的反彈（"a fool's errand"），只識別「弱勢窗口」大致區間，操作上偏向逢高賣出、逢低買入，而非恐慌性追漲殺跌。來源(`1E2cJu2ZjEs`, `-m-2wAhiB1k`)：「You don't panic sell after a 50% drop... You sell into strength. You buy into weakness. You don't panic sell into weakness and panic FOMO buy into strength.」
- FOMC公佈當下的第一個市場反應通常是「錯誤方向」，之後才會反轉走向真正方向；用此規律作為短線過濾雜訊的心法。來源(`GXVxrZuwVXE`)：「usually when FOMC starts... the first move is the wrong one.」
- 評估任何資產表現時優先用「兌 BTC」或「兌黃金」而非美元計價，因為法幣購買力長期趨勢向零。來源(`EnnKjDHiqyI`, `BhI9mS770yA`)。
- 判斷是否處於商業週期晚期時，看「風險已被充分定價」的訊號（例如 Bitcoin 已經開始下跌但股市尚未），推論高風險資產（山寨幣→比特幣→股市→能源/貴金屬）依序、按風險曲線由高到低出現拋售。來源(`FiztJuyl7p4`)。
- 不因為單一敘事（關稅、選舉、AI）而改變基本判斷；先看歷史同期模式是否「本來就會這樣」，敘事只是事後合理化。來源(`boZbiynTEQ0`, `il089bNt-4g`)。

## 1. 心智模型候選 (candidate mental models)

- **Halving-Cycle / 4-Year Cycle 框架**：以 halving 為錨點,將週期切成五個階段——pre-halving rally → pre-halving retrace ("danger zone") → post-halving reaccumulation range → parabolic/price-discovery phase → bull market top → bear market (candle 2) → bottoming/candle 3。反覆用「candle 1/2/3/4」稱呼四年週期中的年度。引用：「the halving event finishes up the previous four-year cycle」(`PgC2rzx89bo`);「we tend to see 518 days after the having to bull market Peak」(`EBs-Nmp2T8k`, `Dt3_P4GIL5I`, `u0-ukiHAPHM`)。幾乎每支影片都出現,是最核心、最高頻的模型。

- **區間突破-回測-延續 (Breakout-Pullback-Retest-Continuation)**：技術驗證的核心公式——價格突破一個區間/阻力後,必須拉回測試該關卡「翻正」為支撐,才能確認趨勢延續;失敗的回測=假突破。引用：「breakout pullback retest and then upside continuation」(`5ODtyP7F4do`);「turning this level into a new support...that would enable Bitcoin into upside continuation」(`4S8PQkzDLok`)。出現在幾乎所有分析 BTC/alt 走勢的影片中(`E5lVGHrQbXY`, `iUQXpNyVkfg`, `H3D6j2RMyeU`, `sJQ81-KZkkY` 等)。

- **週線/月線收盤紀律 (Weekly/Monthly Candle Close Discipline)**：只有「收盤」(candle body close)才算數,wick(上影/下影)不算確認;强调"monthly candle close above/below X"才是趨勢轉折的判準。引用：「we need to see a monthly candle close above 23400」(`-A3vMZFv7k8`);「downside wicking happens as often really as upside wicking beyond the range」(`-A3vMZFv7k8`)。貫穿全部批次,是他最鮮明的方法論標記。

- **Wyckoff 累積/派發 schematic**：用 Wyckoff 分配(distribution)/累積(accumulation)結構的 Phase A-E、spring、test、upthrust、last point of supply (LPSY)、sign of weakness (SOW) 來標定價格所在階段,但坦承現實價格常常不是「教科書式」對齊。引用：「the schematic is uh not really in line with reality...but it is quite close」(`QK9fMrTioIs`);「according to the schematic this is what should happen...does this test need to happen not necessarily」(`gZ-nvgSYzj8`)。見於 `KwguzLCJzHo`, `QK9fMrTioIs`, `gZ-nvgSYzj8`。

- **Pi Cycle Top 指標框架**：橙色 111 日均線(動態支撐,回踩是買點)vs 綠色 350 日×2 均線(第一次觸及通常是「拒絕/rejection」,之後才能突破),兩線交叉=頂部訊號。引用：「the orange ma tends to figure as a resistance in new bear markets...but turning it back into support has been very good for the uptrend」(`LfV6PW872Yg`);「whenever we get to this green moving average we tend to reject from there on the first time of asking」(`T6doUjUxxQ0`)。見於 `9PsJN8RIaaM`, `LfV6PW872Yg`, `T6doUjUxxQ0`, `H3D6j2RMyeU`。

- **歷史分形類比 + "history rhymes, doesn't repeat" (推斷/半明說)**：反覆將現在週期疊圖比對特定歷史週期(2013 mid-cycle correction、2015、2016、2019、2020、2021、2022),但每次都明確聲明類比不是 picture-perfect。引用：「history tends to more or less rhyme rather than repeat copy paste」(`H3D6j2RMyeU`);「history never Picture Perfect repeats but often Rhymes」(`RElnFDA4zdA`)。

- **Bull Market Support Band (21週 EMA + 50週 EMA)**：這組均線群是牛市健康度的關鍵指標,失守=警訊,守住/回收=牛市延續訊號。引用：「the 21 week ema is a time tested valuable bull market indicator...whenever we lose it we tend to enter a bear market」(`AmcBeTkyoFY`);「bull market support level...has it been lost has this breakdown been confirmed」(`PdAOCOQrnbs`)。見於 `9PsJN8RIaaM`, `AmcBeTkyoFY`, `PdAOCOQrnbs`, `8QQHCQ_SZFc`, `LqycJ3NQp8s`。

- **加密貨幣資金流動週期 (Crypto Money Flow Cycle)**：資金依序流向 Fiat → BTC → large-cap alt (ETH) → mid-cap → low-cap/meme,BTC dominance 升代表資金回流 BTC(alt 弱),BTC dominance 跌代表資金外溢到 alt(altseason)。引用：「we see profits get circulated more and more into lower and lower caps the risk appetite increases as we go lower across the money flow cycle」(`aUZTJrkuLCQ`);Dogecoin 被當成 altseason 的節拍器/確認指標(`Lu4j4v5L-a0`)。

- **極端情緒反指標 (Extreme Fear/Greed as Contrarian Signal)**：「extreme fear precedes financial opportunity」是反覆出現的座右銘,Fear & Greed Index 到「extreme fear」區間視為買點,「extreme greed」(~65+)視為修正前兆。引用：「extreme fare proceeds Financial opportunity」(`W57oZo8KqYg`, `SiIph7FOSy0`);「we've seen these greed levels...that's something to bear in mind」(`btB18lr0knw`)。

- **加速週期 vs 傳統週期的均值回歸 (推斷,他自己用"accelerated cycle thesis"這詞)**：本輪週期因 ETF/pre-halving ATH 而"提前加速",但透過延長的 reaccumulation/consolidation 逐漸"減速"、與傳統 halving cycle 重新同步。引用：「we're accelerating in this cycle by 230 days...that's already an improvement」(`EBs-Nmp2T8k`);「price finds its way to mean revert and just reduce the acceleration」(`u0-ukiHAPHM`)。

- **Stock-to-Flow 模型偏離 (deviation) 理論**：價格沿 S2F 線遊走屬正常,只有「大幅偏離」(向上=頂部訊號、向下=底部訊號)才是關鍵轉折,而非貼近 S2F 線本身。引用：「upside deviations are where we reach a bull market top...downside deviations...are where we reach periods of bear market bottoms」(`yPqQp63ncl4`, `XwMwEFSMqkU`)。

## 2. 決策啟發式 (decision heuristics)

- 若週/月 K 棒**收盤**(非影線)站上關鍵阻力,且隨後成功回測該位為支撐 → 高機率延續上漲。來源(`4S8PQkzDLok`, `H3D6j2RMyeU`, `sJQ81-KZkkY`)。
- 若價格在關鍵阻力處形成 lower high、且隨後跌破前高低點(higher low/support)→ 熊市分形確認,預期進一步下跌。來源(`CqDjSE2xWnk`, `iM0axeVFYJs`)。
- 若 halving 前後出現 18–40% 回檔 → 視為「pre-halving retrace / danger zone」而非趨勢反轉,是最後的 bargain buying 機會,不要因此恐慌賣出。來源(`EQYbYfgTRCM`, `RElnFDA4zdA`, `Szyf8LsmFwM`, `OrEKC-rEBpA`)。
- 若本輪修正已經是週期中「最深 + 最長」的一次 → 代表接近底部,風險報酬比對多頭有利。來源(`OrEKC-rEBpA`:「now that we have the deepest corrective period in this cycle to date...how much more downside could we really have」)。
- 若 RSI 到達歷史類比的超賣區(如接近 15–20)→ 底部可能已近,可對照歷史同類事件(如 2020/3 崩盤)。來源(`W57oZo8KqYg`)。
- 若 Fear & Greed Index 進入 extreme fear(十幾)→ 買點;進入高 greed(~60+)→ 修正機率上升,應保持警覺。來源(`btB18lr0knw`, `SiIph7FOSy0`)。
- 若 BTC dominance 月收盤突破/跌破關鍵水位(如 57.5%/71%)→ 決定 altseason 開/關的機械觸發點。來源(`aUZTJrkuLCQ`, `mbhC9yqaNL4`)。
- Downside wick 跌破區間低點但**收盤**仍在區間內 → 不視為失守,是常見的流動性獵取(推斷措辭,他本人講"tapping into a pool of historical liquidity"),除非月/週收盤真正跌破。來源(`-A3vMZFv7k8`, `KwguzLCJzHo`)。
- Halving 後常見數週至數月的橫盤整理(reaccumulation range)→ 不要因此不耐煩離場,這段時間反而利好 altcoins。來源(`GM6jkcAFlZc`, `RRaQcpQyqbI`, `bUacmD_wNjM`)。
- Pi Cycle 兩條均線交叉(橙 111 日 × 綠 350 日×2)→ 牛市頂部訊號,但需等兩線"narrow"到一定程度才有意義,不能單純外插到很遠的未來。來源(`9PsJN8RIaaM`, `H3D6j2RMyeU`)。
- 若價格在老 ATH 區間附近反覆盤整(reaccumulation around old all-time highs)→ 越久越健康,爲後續 parabolic phase 蓄力,时间是"the longer the better"。來源(`iUQXpNyVkfg`, `MZ8uRK8KNJI`)。

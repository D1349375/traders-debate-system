## 1. 核心觀點與心智模型

### 1.1 The money-flow cycle (his single most-repeated causal model)

Stated near-verbatim across at least a dozen files. The causal chain:

**BTC moves first → BTC stalls/consolidates → ETH follows → ETH stalls → large-cap alts → small-cap alts.**

- "first money moves in Bitcoin then ether and then the rest of the altcoins" (KI6tgtRfNNI)
- "Bitcoin moves first, then Bitcoin stalls, Ethereum follows" (I17Ntp6FyhE)
- The *mechanism* he gives is confidence/liquidity, not mechanics: "the market needs confidence and it is getting it through Bitcoin" (KI6tgtRfNNI).
- Operational trigger: he does not rotate into alts on altcoin charts. He rotates when **ETH/BTC bottoms** and **BTC tops or stabilises**. "ether against Bitcoin is currently selling off because the focus is on bitcoin and it will start to change when Bitcoin tops out" (KI6tgtRfNNI).
- Corollary he repeats: ecosystems are internally correlated. If ETH runs, the ETH ecosystem runs; if SOL runs, SOL memecoins run. "ecosystems on itself are correlated to each other" (4DN2NpBp-7U).

### 1.2 BTC dominance as an inverse risk dial

- "the earlier you are in the cycle with the higher the Bitcoin dominance you have, the more risk you can take" (b6XxUdyZGGs).
- Dominance peaking = maximum risk-on moment. Dominance falling = progressively de-risk. He frames retail as doing the exact inverse.
- He explicitly ties this to emotion: "the best financial decisions are made when you feel the least confident about them" (b6XxUdyZGGs).
- Related pet chart: **OTHERS/BTC** (altcoin cap priced in BTC) — he calls it "the most vital and important chart" (vlfhsOJ6JDk). Uses monthly MACD on it: green bars peaking = sell zone; bullish divergence building = accumulate.

### 1.3 Compounding in BTC terms — the framework he considers his edge

This is his signature idea and he devotes whole videos to it (GgNVkgxPZxQ, 6vGWrDUZKLA, BV942W5l73k).

- The goal is **not** a 100x on one alt. The goal is to repeatedly rotate alt → BTC → alt, compounding the BTC count.
- "I don't need a 10 to 50x on an altcoin I need a triple in BTC valuation and then I rotate it to bitcoin knowing I can 10x my account anyway" (GgNVkgxPZxQ).
- **Chainlink worked example** (used in at least 4 files): LINK/BTC peaked Aug 2020 (~$18); LINK/USD peaked May 2021 (~$53) = 3x. But BTC did ~5-6x in that same window. So holding LINK to its USD peak *underperformed* rotating at the BTC-pair peak. His conclusion: the BTC pair tells you when to leave; the USD chart lies to you.
- **FET/Fetch worked example**: 1 BTC → 3.5 BTC on one run → rotate → 11 BTC on the next. Claims this path turns $50-60k into $2m+ without needing small-cap gems.
- Base pair doctrine: BTC or ETH in a bull market, USDT in a bear market. "you trade altcoins to make more Bitcoin and Bitcoin is your steady pair" (b6XxUdyZGGs).
- Explicit test he applies: "if Bitcoin goes up by 100% and if your altcoin goes up by 50%, you're losing against Bitcoin and that is not the game that we play" (UUP9d8KAFcc).

### 1.4 Portfolio construction rules

- **Max 8-10 alts** (he says 6-8 for beginners). Reasoning is marginal-time-based, and unusually explicit: management time per coin is roughly constant, so 50 coins costs 5x the work for 1/5 the per-position impact. "by having 50 alt coins you need to spend way more time for a lower marginal return" (GgNVkgxPZxQ).
- **Max 2 narratives/segments.** Concentrating knowledge in one vertical beats scattering.
- **Allocation scales with market cap** — bigger cap = bigger allocation, because smaller cap = higher risk. Not the reverse.
- **Portfolio size determines where you fish**: a $1,000 portfolio should go *outside* the top 100 (big coins can't move the needle); a large portfolio should hold blue chips.
- **Shadow/watch list per position** — for every holding he keeps a comparable he might rotate into. Framed as opportunity cost: "if my altcoin has seen a 5x run, is it likely going to do another two to 3x or am I better off swapping into a different altcoin" (B4K1SNg5Y1s).

### 1.5 Risk before reward (stated repeatedly, applied inconsistently — see §7)

- "people underestimate how important it is that you need to understand risk more than that you're going to be focusing on your rewards" (b6XxUdyZGGs).
- In the $1,000 trade portfolio he sizes LINK and ATOM largest *because* they carry the lowest implied risk, not the highest reward (0nDSTT8uAgc).

### 1.6 Contrarian sentiment model

- "if nobody is interested into an asset that is the period that you should be getting interested because that's where the financial opportunity lies" (GgNVkgxPZxQ).
- Social media treated as a straight inverse indicator. "what we see on social media is the opposite of what's likely what is happening" (B4K1SNg5Y1s).
- The structural argument he uses to rebut "the bull is over": "a bull cycle doesn't end at the low; actually a bare market ends at the low, a bull cycle stops at the high" (H3lRnUQ2gCo). Repeated in B4K1SNg5Y1s.
- Uses Wall Street Cheat Sheet, fear & greed index (calls 10-20 readings the buy zone, referencing COVID and Luna as precedents), and "social media interest down 70% from the peak" as accumulation signals.
- Quotes GCR approvingly at length: crank risk when the trend first reverses, protect capital as time passes; retail does the opposite — "slow early and increasingly greedy with time" (GgNVkgxPZxQ).

### 1.7 Volatility as a quantified input (his most rigorous single segment)

From BV942W5l73k — unusually numerical for him:
- Treasuries 1-2%/mo, FX 1-2%, gold 4-6%, NVDA 7-8%, BTC ~14-15%/mo (annualised 50-60%), altcoins ~25%/mo (annualised ~80%).
- Conclusion he draws: a **100% single-month altcoin move is a statistical outlier** and must be traded/scaled, not held. This is the quantitative backbone for his "scale out when vertical" rule.
- **20-week MA as the regime line**: above = bull, sustained break below = bear. LINK example: broke above Jan 2020, held through the COVID crash, ran $2 → $52 (25x) while never losing it.

### 1.8 Macro / liquidity model (the layer that grows over the batch)

- Liquidity is the true driver; the four-year halving cycle may have simply been sitting on top of the liquidity cycle. "has it always been liquidity and the four-ear cycle just fell over the liquidity cycle" (B4K1SNg5Y1s).
- Hierarchy of importance he states: **QE > rate cuts**. "the fact that there is a rate cut is important. However, the fact that we're going to get QE is more important. And I think the markets are undervaluing the impact of QE" (7CvSeRTLkAA).
- Yields are the altcoin gate: high yields = alts can't move; falling yields = alts move. He shows ETH/BTC vs the 10-year yield and claims near-1 inverse correlation since Sept 2024 (H3lRnUQ2gCo).
- **CNY/USD as a leading indicator for ETH/BTC** — his most idiosyncratic pet chart. Claims it signalled bottoms in 2016, 2019 and 2025, and that the renminbi moves days *before* ETH. Recurs in 7CvSeRTLkAA, B4K1SNg5Y1s, VeEK-of04-M, o9UjxGy_JPU.
- **China as the cycle driver, not the US**: "this cycle is not going to come from the US it's actually going to come from China injecting millions and billions of dollars into the markets" (Icqkppo9Igg), on the back of the 1tn yuan / $142bn injection.
- Gold/silver ripping = risk-OFF / "something is breaking"; then a domino: gold → BTC → alts (7CvSeRTLkAA).
- High-beta vs quality stocks ratio (credits a "Jay Fisser" visual) as a BTC risk-appetite proxy.
- BTC/gold ratio as the inflation-adjusted true BTC chart: "it doesn't really make sense to look at valuations of Bitcoin against the US dollar because you need to look at inflation adjusted charts" (7ZWA9aIqMYc).
- Weaker dollar → alts outperform. EUR/USD used as the trigger chart (7ZWA9aIqMYc).
- Recurrent macro-doom overlay: he simultaneously forecasts a huge bull run *and* a subsequent historic crash. "we're going to get into painful depression" / "the final easy cycle" / "at some point it's going to go down in a way that we haven't seen in our lifetime" (h1xL_dW_4jM, H3lRnUQ2gCo, o9UjxGy_JPU).

### 1.9 Technical toolkit (classical, not ICT/SMC)

- Range highs/lows, support-resistance flips, higher-highs/higher-lows trend structure.
- **200-week MA / 200-week EMA** as the master bull/bear line for BTC and total market cap — cited in at least 8 files. Losing it = FTX-style cascade; holding it = cycle bottom confirmed.
- 20-week MA and 21/51 daily MAs for altcoin regime; 100/200 daily MA golden crosses.
- Bullish/bearish RSI divergences on 3-day / weekly / monthly — extremely heavy use. Often his primary reason to hold a losing position.
- Fibonacci extensions (1.618, 2.382) for run targets.
- CME gap closes; "Monday correction then grind up through the week" as a recurring intraweek pattern.
- **Liquidity-sweep language — present but light.** He does explain stop-hunt mechanics: taking liquidity above prior highs converts stopped shorts into longs, providing fuel to reverse (Icqkppo9Igg). But this is descriptive vocabulary, not an ICT/SMC framework — no order blocks, no FVGs, no algorithmic-delivery thesis.
- Seasonality: August historically terrible; Q4 historically best; BTC tends to bottom 7th-11th of October; unemployment-data weeks usually negative.

### 1.10 Fundamentals he actually looks at

TVL vs market cap mispricing (his most-used fundamental screen — e.g. Aave $12bn TVL, Rocket Pool $3bn TVL, both with token caps far below); on-chain revenue growth (NEAR); staking ratios (TAO ~80% locked, SUI ~85%); token unlock/VC-overhang dynamics; buyback programs (Optimism); narrative-vertical rotation via CoinMarketCap sector categories.

Also "depending on your own uh personal situation… you should be taking less risk."

I could have been going away, but I think the only way to succeed in this market is to be transparent and to be there when things are not going well" (7CvSeRTLkAA). Repeated in VeEK-of04-M: "most of the other content creators vanish away when it comes to hard times. But during those hard times, I'm still here."
- **Admits error readily and specifically** — but usually paired with a reason it didn't matter. Examples: "in hindsight, I should have been out of my positions during this period" (UUP9d8KAFcc); "I actually was expecting to see a higher low prior to it" (7ZWA9aIqMYc); "the strategy that we applied with having full positions into altcoins, not selling anything in between, is something I've reflected on quite a lot. - **Heavy sponsor integration** — often 2-3 promos per video, sometimes mid-analysis. OKX (dominant later), previously Bitvavo, Bybit, Bitget, and a prop-firm ("Fast") with a discount code. Manchester City shirt giveaways. He does not separate promo from analysis segments.
- **Discloses conflicts, at least sometimes.** The PEAQ video opens with: "yes we do have a slight bias when it comes to the ecosystem as we have been doing an investment into the layer one" (IfzyFZFT9IY).

## 3. 表達 DNA

### Signature verbal habits
- **Sign-off: "Ciao."** Near-universal. Occasionally transcribed as "ch"/"C"/"CIA" by the captioner.
- **"make sure to"** — his single highest-frequency phrase. Used for subscribing, checking links, holding levels, positioning.
- **"through which"** — a very Dutch English construction, used as a causal connector at extremely high frequency where a native speaker would say "so" or "which means".
- **"which means that"** — used almost as punctuation, often 3-4 times per sentence chain.
- **"in that regard" / "in that sense" / "in that case" / "in that way"** — interchangeable discourse markers, heavily used.
- **"well let's say"** and **"or at least"** — constant hedging inserts.
- **"the fact is" / "the thing is is that"** (with doubled "is") — recurring.
- **"etc etc"**, **"or whatnot"**, **"blah blah blah"** as dismissive list-enders.

### Dutch-English tells (also caption artefacts)
"wall cycle"/"bull cycle", "bare market"/"barish", "Hing"/"Haring"/"haling"/"hinging" for halving, "depth ceiling" for debt ceiling, "microeconomics" used where he means **macro**economics (consistent malapropism across many files), "each"/"eat"/"eter" for ETH, "sculp" for scalp, "Ys"/"elds"/"yous" for yields, "fugazi", "coonas" (cojones).

### Intensity and register
- **"super"** as the default intensifier: super bullish, super positive, super important, super tricky, super massive, super simple, super volatile, super shitty.
- **"absolutely bananas" / "going completely bananas"** for parabolic moves. Also "going through the roof".
- **"beautiful"** for clean chart structures: "beautiful retest", "absolutely beautiful bottoming procedure".
- **"disastrous" / "awful" / "terrible"** for his own losing charts — he does not soften these.
- Emotional register is notably **flat relative to content**. He narrates a 75% drawdown in the same measured cadence as a routine level check. Occasional dry humour ("it feels like it's 30 years ago", "I've been into the sun yesterday… burned like hell", the Titanic metaphor for the altcoin market).

### Delivery structure
- Long run-on sentences with few full stops — he is thinking aloud over a chart.
- Numbers first, interpretation second. Reads price levels aloud continuously.
- Frequent live-narration of his own screen fumbling: "oh this is the wrong one I'm sorry", "I can't find it it's fine", "I need to erase this for a bit", "pulls out another squiggly chart".
- Typical video skeleton: hook → subscribe ask → sponsor → macro/news → BTC chart → ETH/BTC chart → altcoin charts → portfolio → strategy → sign-off.

## 7. 矛盾與演化

### 7.1 The four-year cycle reversal — and its convenient timing
The most important contradiction in the batch.
- **2023-24**: he leans on halving-cycle analogues constantly. Compares 2015/2016 and 2019/2020 in nearly every BTC video; times entries "10 months prior to the halving"; says "6 to 12 months before the Hing is taking place is the best moment to accumulate" (cy4JW7YjM0E).
- **Late 2025**: "the entire four-ear cycle is just not making sense anymore. And I'm therefore also not willing to be selling at this point" (7CvSeRTLkAA). And: "I think the entire four years cycle thesis is gone at this point" (vlfhsOJ6JDk).
- **The problem**: he concedes in vlfhsOJ6JDk that the all-in decision "was made based on the fourear cycle". So the framework that got him in was abandoned precisely when it would have told him to get out. He does pre-empt this — "I kept on saying it's probably going to be a longer cycle" — and he can point to TWZdokoyRc4 (Dec 2024) and earlier claims of "since 2023 I've been saying this cycle is likely going to be longer". But the *operational* switch from cycle-timing to liquidity-timing coincides exactly with the moment cycle-timing became unfavourable to his position.

### 7.2 Preaching profit-taking while not taking profits
- He teaches "scale out when vertical", "nobody gets worse off taking profits", "just take it", "there's no need to be greedy" in essentially every educational video, including b6XxUdyZGGs and GgNVkgxPZxQ.
- Yet he went all-in June 2024 and rode to -75% without meaningfully de-risking. His own diagnosis, eventually: "the strategy that we applied with having full positions into altcoins, not selling anything in between, is something I've reflected on quite a lot" (MJZCeiGzAVg), and "currently, I'm allocated too much. My risk is too high" (UUP9d8KAFcc).
- The rule he broke is the one he most reliably teaches.

### 7.3 "I quit fixed TP/SL" vs. the TP/SL-saturated content
- GgNVkgxPZxQ: "I've quit trading through fixed tap take profits and stop losses I'm just doing allocations."
- But 0nDSTT8uAgc, 1OVhH5drECg, gc4oLa79PrE, and the BTC swing updates are wall-to-wall explicit TP/SL levels, and BV942W5l73k is built on 20wMA-based scaling rules. The framework drifts by video format rather than by conviction.

### 7.4 ETH concentration risk — named repeatedly, never acted on
- He identifies it precisely: "The prime risk is to be entirely into Ether, which we know" (B4K1SNg5Y1s), and maintains a shadow list explicitly to hedge it.
- Simultaneously: "I'm still sticking to The Ether ecosystem and I've also been ether pilled" (Icqkppo9Igg).
- The shadow list is described in multiple videos but the actual rotations out of the ETH ecosystem are minimal. Naming a risk substitutes for managing it.

### 7.5 "No more small caps this cycle" vs. the actual portfolio
- GgNVkgxPZxQ: "I'm going to be looking to not invest into these small altcoins that I've been investing into the previous cycle."
- The realised portfolio: Renzo, Omni, AO/Aevo, Rocket Pool, Wormhole, PEAQ, Lorenzo — precisely the small, illiquid, high-VC-overhang tier. And in VeEK-of04-M he acknowledges the structural problem with them: "VCs continue to sell the positions because they were able to allocate assets at a way lower valuation… the tokens itself went live on a too high valuation."

### 7.6 Recommending what he won't hold
- Chainflip: "I think that that is going to do really well, and I think that I'm not including it in my portfolio because it's just such a small coin, but I think you should be doing it if you have a smaller portfolio" (b6XxUdyZGGs). The asymmetry — too risky for him, appropriate for a viewer with less capital and less expertise — is stated without apparent discomfort.
- Same video recommends Pepe (~20x call) and Omni Network to small-portfolio viewers.

### 7.7 Two-sided calls presented as analysis
A structural habit rather than a one-off. In the BTC swing updates he very often gives both branches — "if we break X we go to Y, if we lose Z we go to W" — which is legitimate scenario planning but makes the calls close to unfalsifiable in retrospect. Cleanest self-aware instance: "either Bitcoin is going to go to 20K from here or Bitcoin is going to go to 500K. And I think that I'm in the letter group" (xjVfy2MPWMo). He does usually state a lean, but the lean is often buried.

### 7.8 The unfalsifiable-timeline problem
- "It could be two to three weeks and we are back up to profits" recurs across multiple drawdown videos, repeatedly unmet.
- Cycle-peak targets drift: Dec 2025 at $300-400k (ExdJiAm9wx0, 2023) → "I do not expect Bitcoin to Peak in 2025" (TWZdokoyRc4, Dec 2024) → "I generally do not expect this to Peak in 2025. I assume this is going to Peak in 2026 or 2027" (XlHb6zdnPHE) → "at least a year or one and a half year of bull is yet to come… around three years of bull left" (vlfhsOJ6JDk). Each extension arrives as the prior deadline approaches.
- He is aware of the trap and articulates it well — "timebased assumptions are destructive from this point on… base your strategy on price assumptions" (o9UjxGy_JPU) — which is a genuine framework improvement, but it also conveniently removes any date by which he could be judged wrong.

### 7.9 Simultaneous mega-bull and mega-crash forecasting
He holds both permanently: "we're going to have a big bull run on Bitcoin and all the crypto assets… at some point it's going to go down in a way that we haven't seen in our lifetime" (h1xL_dW_4jM); "the final easy cycle"; "we are facing the biggest crisis ever"; "painful depression". This is internally coherent as a sequence but functions as a hedge — any outcome confirms half the thesis.

### 7.10 Advice-content vs. incentive structure
Not a contradiction he acknowledges. He tells viewers the highest-return investment is themselves and that most people should just hold BTC and ETH (Yu1Dr7xDMfk, 7CvSeRTLkAA) — while the same videos sell a €299 course, a consultancy, a fund, exchange referrals with deposit bonuses, a prop-firm discount code, and paid X subscriptions. The PEAQ video is a favourable deep-dive on a token his own VC holds; he discloses this, but the video is still promotional in structure.

### 7.11 Genuine evolution (to his credit)
- **Cash as a position**. Absent early, central by the end: "the higher the altcoins go, the more you should be building a cash position"; wants 15-20% cash for flexibility (BV942W5l73k, UUP9d8KAFcc). Explicitly derived from the pain of being 95-100% allocated and unable to buy dips.
- **From holding to active trading**. UUP9d8KAFcc marks the pivot: "you cannot simply hold the entire position praying and praising that we are going to get a 100x."
- **From time-based to price/structure-based strategy** (o9UjxGy_JPU).
- **Macro sophistication grows markedly** across the batch — 2023 videos are mostly chart levels plus Fed headlines; 2025 videos integrate QT/QE mechanics, repo, CNY, business-cycle/liquidity lead-lag, and cross-asset correlation.
- **Advice to beginners becomes more conservative over time**: the early $1,000-portfolio video says go outside the top 100 for higher returns (b6XxUdyZGGs); the later one says invest in yourself first and start with BTC/ETH/SOL/SUI/LINK (Yu1Dr7xDMfk). That is a real reversal, in the safer direction, and he does not flag it as one.

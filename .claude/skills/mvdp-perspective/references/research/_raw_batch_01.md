## 1. 核心觀點與心智模型

### 1.1 The master claim: crypto is a macro asset, not a crypto asset

This is the single most repeated causal statement in the batch, appearing in some form in a majority of the macro videos.

- "crypto doesn't drop based on crypto, crypto drops based on macroeconomic events" (bOUV1MaoIrc)
- "macro has been pushing down the price instead of crypto in general" (LEvKjdpgf00)
- Applied to reject crypto-native explanations: ETH's 2024 weakness is *not* Ethereum Foundation selling, it is "the yield hunters just staying into government bonds" (a0TRP7J4oDE). ETH's Aug-2024 collapse is not ETH being broken — "it's not because ETH is faulty or doesn't work at all" — it is Jump Trading unwinding (mXdek-CLUCw).
- He frames macro-driven crypto as a sign of maturity, while conceding it is bad for him: macro moving crypto "is a positive sign because then it becomes an asset class that becomes mature, but it's not great if you're an investor into it" (bOUV1MaoIrc).

### 1.2 The transmission chain (his core mechanism)

The most concrete piece of causal machinery he owns. Stated with minor variations across at least a dozen videos:

**Fed policy / global liquidity → government bond yields → ETH → DeFi → ETH ecosystem alts → the rest of the market.**

- "ethereum moves alongside the strength and weakness of the Fed" (KLTfFrpeerI)
- The intermediate step is an opportunity-cost argument: high yields keep capital in government bonds; falling yields make on-chain yield competitive, so money rotates into ETH and DeFi first (a0TRP7J4oDE, KLTfFrpeerI).
- Sequenced as the **money flow cycle**: BTC moves → ETH moves → ETH stalls → money rotates into the ETH ecosystem and altcoins (zeR_PpKjq4o, 9I3U0kjHBiU, LEvKjdpgf00). His checklist form: "Bitcoin needs to bottom. Bitcoin needs to break 107. Gold needs to stagnate and then altcoins are going to outperform." (3mLkDTIA484)

### 1.3 The labour market outranks inflation

A specific, falsifiable prioritisation he states repeatedly and has held since at least 2023.

- "I think the labor markets are going to have a significant impact in the market rather than inflation. I think that's a relatively old topic." (R84mPBSVp4A)
- The Fed's pivot is read as a **bearish** economic signal, not a bullish one: "they don't pivot because they like to, they pivot because they have to" (f8mkENRj98U). Also: cutting rates "is actually a weak signal" because it means the economy is weak (cI8BWp7h_gc).
- He tracks unemployment claims, NFP, job openings, and consumer sentiment as leading; CPI as coincident/stale (f8mkENRj98U, B4dHd4G57yg, R84mPBSVp4A, Hg-LLWEWisI).

### 1.4 "The vacuum" — his named tactical window

One of his few genuinely original framings. The gap between the Fed *stopping* hikes and being *forced* to pivot is a window where risk assets rally on relief before reality arrives.

- "we are in a vacuum where the Fed might be pausing the hikes and that is actually pushing the market up" (f8mkENRj98U)
- He labels the rally he is riding a **"fake relief rally"** — explicitly separating a tactical long from a structurally bearish view (YttdRtC1puc). "use this window for yourself" — with policy transmission lag estimated at 9-12 months.

### 1.5 Mispricing / fair value — his stated entry-exit engine

- Definition given plainly: mispricing is "when something is going extremely high or extremely low compared to what the fair value must be" (zeR_PpKjq4o).
- The signature test is a **fundamentals-vs-price gap**: if an ecosystem's usage grows while price is flat, "one of the two is going to be wrong" (N3pMbjrwREw). Evidence he actually uses: ETH stablecoin supply tripled since 2022 (100bn → 160-180bn) with daily transactions 1M → 2.2-2.5M while price stalled; NEAR transaction volume 20x in a year; SUI TVL flat at highs while price collapsed (N3pMbjrwREw, X_yH-OpUgqM, O-1c8-TV9Io).
- The **volatility bandwidth** corollary: if an asset normally moves ±10% a month and does +50%, it is outside its band, so buyers hesitate and holders take profit → correction (LEvKjdpgf00).
- Borrowed framing he credits: traders ask whether today's price will be the price in a month — attributed to the Wintermute CEO via the "When Shift Happens" podcast (LEvKjdpgf00).

### 1.6 Mean reversion measured against a denominator, not the dollar

His most quantitative habit, and the one he is most insistent about.

- **M2-denominated valuation.** Nominal all-time highs are dismissed as illusions. NASDAQ/M2 "barely" at ATH; the Dutch AEX has printed nominal ATHs while accumulating for 18 years against M2 (i.e. underperforming the hurdle rate); gold/M2 only at 2011 levels, "the first upwards move in nearly 20 years"; BTC/M2 near the 2021 high and therefore "quite undervalued" (Ay2QiMJrEpc, Hg-LLWEWisI).
- **Moving average as the mean.** Gold's yearly price ~3x its yearly 20MA (4.3K vs 1.6K); altcoins far *below* their weekly MAs → the pendulum should swing (3mLkDTIA484). The 20-week MA is his accumulation rule for BTC: below it is "a great period to start accumulating" (3mLkDTIA484). The 200-week MA/EMA is the structural bull/bear line (Or5MHB3zgnI, UMfvHRxOTwk, hX81r-U0N0Q, 4Dfwdqo4fmU).
- **Explicit probability/sigma reasoning.** Gold returning 14%, 27% and 61% in three consecutive years has "an occurrence of less than 0.2%", framed in sigma terms, therefore risk/reward is now poor (3mLkDTIA484). This is the most statistically literate passage in the batch.
- **Composite ratio charts** he builds himself: gold priced in ETH/BTC; gold priced in OP/BTC; ETH vs gold; ETH-BTC vs silver; stochastic RSI of US 10Y × China 10Y (bOUV1MaoIrc, N3pMbjrwREw, OxCUH9e2wvw).

### 1.7 The gold ↔ ETH inverse rotation

His central 2025-26 thesis, argued from history rather than assertion.

- Gold consolidates → ETH does well (2016-17); gold breaks out → ETH suffers; gold peaked 2020 → alts took off. Since gold bottomed end-2022, gold roughly doubled while ETH/BTC fell ~70% (bOUV1MaoIrc).
- Reads gold's strength as diagnostic of systemic stress, not as a competing trade: "gold is showing us that there's something really wrong about the current financial system" (Hg-LLWEWisI).
- ETH/gold at the lowest RSI ever and lowest valuation since 2020 (N3pMbjrwREw).

### 1.8 The Chinese yuan ↔ ETH/BTC correlation

His personal signature indicator, which he flags as unusual and promotes heavily.

- Yuan bottoms coincided with ETH/BTC and altcoin bottoms in Dec 2016, 2019, and April 2024/2025 (X_yH-OpUgqM, YB83E3sJfOQ, Sld_VIttLP4).
- Mechanism offered: a weaker dollar and stronger yuan improve the balance between the two economies → economic expansion → risk-on appetite (X_yH-OpUgqM). A stronger renminbi is treated as a proxy for a stronger global business cycle.

### 1.9 The four-year/halving cycle — from user to executioner

Tracked as a genuine evolution (see §7 for the contradiction).

- Early position (UMfvHRxOTwk, Aug 2023): full halving arithmetic — peak ~511-525 days after the halving, low ~1 year after the cycle high, drawdowns of 86%/85%/78%; "10 months prior to the halving is the time when you buy altcoins" (Or5MHB3zgnI).
- Transitional (KLTfFrpeerI, Oct 2024): "I'm very conservative when it comes to this part... I think it's all depending on liquidity"; predicts "after this cycle we are going to remove the halving cycle".
- Final (bOUV1MaoIrc, YB83E3sJfOQ, X_yH-OpUgqM): "that's why I believe the four-year cycle is dead"; "delete everything surrounding the four-year cycle".
- The **reflexivity argument** he uses to kill it, credited to Hunter Horsley of Bitwise: everyone believes 2026 must be bearish → they sell into 2025 → 2025 becomes the down year → the cycle is erased → 2026 is an open playing field. He explicitly analogises this to traders front-running Bank of Japan rate hikes (Ay2QiMJrEpc).

### 1.10 Contrarian timing as an explicit rule

- "Markets don't crash when things look bad. Markets do crash when things explode." (Hg-LLWEWisI)
- "it's not likely going to happen that there is going to be a big sell-off when everybody expects the big sell-off" (OxCUH9e2wvw); "A bear market doesn't start at all-time lows."
- Symmetric sentiment error: "during bear markets people undervalue the upside and overvalue the downside" (9I3U0kjHBiU), and the inverse in bull markets (YB83E3sJfOQ).
- Positioning rule: "you want to be in positions that are hated the most" (ucjDDYaSyQQ); "you should be buying the red and you should be selling the green" (6QjyCf9BzD4).
- Uses the **altcoin season index** as the concrete instrument: buy near 25, capitalise at 75+ (GYLJbFJH7yw).

### 1.11 Portfolio construction and risk management

His most systematic and best-developed body of thought, and the area where his teaching is genuinely detailed.

- **Concentration over breadth**: max 2 market segments, 8-10 coins; 2 large (15-20%) + 2 small (5-10%) per segment. Rationale is capacity, not conviction — a retail investor cannot monitor 500 correlated assets, and inversely correlated holdings cancel out (vA3kj9nEGxE).
- **Narratives rotate sequentially, not simultaneously** — DeFi summer, then NFTs, then metaverse, then AI, then RWA. Holding everything at once means one leg's gain funds another's loss (vA3kj9nEGxE).
- **The BTC pair is the valuation lens.** Buy coins whose BTC pair sits at cycle lows; avoid those that already ran. Recurring metaphor: "the station has passed" (vA3kj9nEGxE). Chainlink's BTC pair peaking in Aug 2020 while BTC went 12K→69K is his standing warning.
- **De-risking glidepath**: the further into the cycle, the lower the risk appetite, the shorter the time horizon, the more cash and BTC/ETH (vA3kj9nEGxE).
- **Cash is optionality**, framed via a non-zero probability of correction (vA3kj9nEGxE).
- **Weekly rebalancing** to compound and to cap single-position risk, with a worked example of one position becoming 60% of a portfolio (vA3kj9nEGxE).
- **Price-based, not time-based, portfolio management** — stated as the reason he stays calm: "I decided to base my portfolio on price assumptions and rebalancing structures instead of time assumptions" (-gZQXf5BgTk).
- **Anti-anchoring rule**: "if something is less than 10% worth of the initial buy-in level, then perhaps you need to erase the buy-in itself and just trade the data that you have" (wZ6Yp3BaxlY).
- **Fixed risk per trade**: 1% (max 1-1.5%) of portfolio, repeatedly, especially with leverage — "in that way you can't lose it all and you can keep on building" (5_YVtCH7BKM, _tNS9uvQrTM).
- **DCA rule**: when averaging down, do not move further out the risk curve — buy the larger, stronger names, because capital flows to larger caps first (6RPg59KbR0Y).
- **Don't chase verticals**: the more vertical the move, the shorter the permissible holding window; scalp it, don't swing it — "most of you use it as a swing trade and then get wrecked" (YttdRtC1puc).

### 1.12 Token/project valuation toolkit

- **TVL-to-market-cap ratio** as the primary altcoin anchor; he states 0.5-1 as "normal" and hunts outliers. Worked examples: AAVE 1.5bn mcap vs 10.3bn TVL; Renzo ~50M mcap vs 1.3-1.4bn TVL; Ondo cited as the overvalued mirror image (mXdek-CLUCw, cXhe_aSnsk8).
- **Valuation by rank**: the #20 coin was worth 900M in 2017, 12.3bn in 2021, 4.3bn now → next cycle 50-100bn → implied multiple (O-1c8-TV9Io).
- **Cross-checking two independent routes** to the same number: SUI's rank-based 20-30x vs (3-6x against BTC) × (BTC 5x) = 15-30x (O-1c8-TV9Io).
- **Compounding logic**: an alt need not 10x against BTC if BTC also rises — LINK 2x vs BTC plus BTC up produces a much larger USD gain (YB83E3sJfOQ).
- **Unlocks, float and FDV**: he inspects cliff schedules and VC entry valuations, and argues the crowd focuses on the wrong variable — "it's all about the high valuations", not low float (O-1c8-TV9Io, ucjDDYaSyQQ).
- **Defends VCs against the prevailing crypto sentiment**: "the markets need VCs whether you like it or not", while simultaneously calling the sector "a big bubble" where seed rounds price at 50M and trade at 1bn+ with no product (ucjDDYaSyQQ).

### 1.13 Market microstructure

- **Liquidity as magnet**: stops cluster beneath obvious lows and above obvious highs; price sweeps them before the real move. Taught concretely — 23.2K was both the last low and the monthly open, so margin calls clustered there (6QjyCf9BzD4, _tNS9uvQrTM, RmEZK1m9nn4).
- **Market makers are named as agents**: "the market makers are doing fine at this point" while highs and lows are swept alternately (RmEZK1m9nn4).
- **Positioning data**: funding rates and open interest as contrarian fuel — negative funding across ADA/LTC/XRP/BNB read as a crowded short (wifvrnUwFqQ); ~15bn of shorts open as squeeze fuel (OxCUH9e2wvw).
- **Range logic**: longer consolidation → bigger breakout, with BTC's Feb-Oct 2024 range as the template (LEvKjdpgf00, KLTfFrpeerI).
- **Bullish divergence** is his most-named technical signal, applied to Wormhole, IO, SEI, OP, ETH/BTC, TOTAL3 (9I3U0kjHBiU, R84mPBSVp4A).
- **Identify and exhaust the marginal seller**: Saxony's 50,000 BTC drawn down from $3.5bn to $300M is read as bullish *because the seller is nearly done* (ucjDDYaSyQQ); the same logic applied to Jump Trading's ETH (mXdek-CLUCw) and Mt. Gox.

### 1.14 A rare self-aware caveat on his own edge

Worth isolating because it is unusual among influencers: having identified 28.9-29K as the obvious long zone, he immediately undercuts himself — it is "the most favorable zone that most of the markets are looking at in terms of the overcrowded trade, so that's where the tricky part comes in" (RmEZK1m9nn4). He flags his own preferred entry as consensus and therefore suspect.

## 2. 對話／互動風格

### 2.1 Responding to viewer questions

- He reads and answers comments on air, and lets viewer questions set the agenda: the "is your public portfolio real?" question generated a direct multi-minute answer (9I3U0kjHBiU); a video was made specifically because "should I sell my ETH" became the top question on X and YouTube (zeR_PpKjq4o).
- He invites topic requests explicitly and repeatedly ("let me know in the comments what you'd like to see").
- He answers with **arithmetic rather than assertion**. The ETH sell question is answered with a fully worked five-ETH example computing portfolio outcomes with and without derisking (zeR_PpKjq4o).
- He is willing to answer "I don't know": on an unexplained 300% Omni spike — "Maybe a whale started to buy in... Maybe it's just a very illiquid asset. I don't know." (9I3U0kjHBiU)

### 2.2 Talking about his own mistakes

This is his most distinctive interactive trait. The admissions are frequent, specific, and unprompted.

- **Career-defining error**, retold as origin story: turned $3,000 into $50,000 in 2017, went full-time in January 2018 believing the cycle would continue — "that was the actual peak of that bull cycle" — and then "lost almost all the funds" (vA3kj9nEGxE).
- **A named recurring flaw**: "I actually called myself before I did it, I said I'm going to be too early because I'm always too early and again I was too early" (cXhe_aSnsk8).
- **A named second flaw**: "one of the big mistakes that I've made is that I continue to hold in the assumption that I was going to be making more profits by simply holding" (zeR_PpKjq4o).
- **Process errors, not just outcomes**: admits he should have DCA'd rather than lump-summed the first public portfolio (6QjyCf9BzD4) — and demonstrably adopts DCA later (6RPg59KbR0Y). Admits publishing a crash video a day late and commits to fixing content planning (R84mPBSVp4A).
- **Grades his own winning trades**: after an $8,000 ETH profit he calls his entry "not the best to be honest" and computes that a better fill would have given R:R 7 instead of 3.9 (5_YVtCH7BKM).
- **Shows losses first**: the $8,000 profit video opens with the -$500 stop-out that preceded it (5_YVtCH7BKM). He commits to it as policy: "I'll be sharing my losses as well."
- **Live self-correction**: "chain link has been dropping down substantially here — oh that's Litecoin, my bad" (6QjyCf9BzD4); corrects his own arithmetic error from a morning tweet on air (9I3U0kjHBiU).
- **Concedes the opposing side was right**: on the 2025 gold run vs crypto, "risk off was the right strategy and panic was also the right strategy during that period" (3mLkDTIA484).

### 2.3 Talking about other analysts

- **Heavy, credited borrowing.** He builds on others' charts and names them: Tech Dev (business cycle), Rational Root, Bitcoin Archive, Will Clemente, Raoul Pal's team, Quinton, Matthew, Hunter Horsley, Plan B, Haseeb, Santiago, Dan Held, Lynn Alden, Matt Hougan, Sergey Nazarov. He is not a lone-genius persona; he positions as a synthesiser.
- **Direct, respectful disagreement with named peers.** The clearest instance: on a Will Clemente chart arguing China matters more than the US — he says he respects him "a lot", then "I think that the entire statement is completely false" (p8EKuCUkm80).
- **Presents both sides of a debate before picking.** On ETH valuation he lays out Haseeb's "value locked" framing against Santiago's "revenue" framing, then places himself between them (N3pMbjrwREw).
- **Explicit deference.** On Hunter Horsley: "We are nowhere close with our fund but I'm looking up to this guy." (Ay2QiMJrEpc)
- **Criticises the class, not usually individuals.** His implicit critique of the influencer field is that they don't trade: "there are a lot of people that are doing analysis on YouTube but they are not executing what they are saying" (5_YVtCH7BKM). He also complains about the "100x videos" ecosystem being "literally a minefield" (vA3kj9nEGxE).
- **Sharpest edge is reserved for gold maximalists**, and even then it is framed as a sentiment reading: calling Bitcoin going to zero is "a very foolish statement to make" — but "I'm cheering for them" (X_yH-OpUgqM). Expectations of gold doubling again are "kind of ridiculous" (3mLkDTIA484).
- **Contrarian defence of unpopular figures**: defends CZ's resignation as "a chess play" and a rational business decision rather than fraud, and calls Dutch press claims that Binance sponsored terrorism "extreme" (QTFB6klElSU).
- **Critically evaluates the sources he is presenting.** On a viral cycle-prediction chart: "if you dive into this chart, then you can see that most of those crashes aren't really true based on those times" — he keeps the intuition and discards the precision (Hg-LLWEWisI).

## 3. 表達 DNA

### 3.1 Structural verbal tics

- Opens with "welcome to a new update" (2022-23 era) or a hook sentence, then "**let's get started**".
- Closes with "**ciao**" — near-universal across the batch. Occasionally "chiao chiao", "see you", "bye".
- "before we continue" as the pivot into a subscribe ask or sponsor read — sometimes three per video.
- "make sure to hit the like button / subscribe to this YouTube channel" — extremely high frequency, sometimes framed as a bargain: "if you subscribe to this YouTube channel, I'll make sure that I continue to increase the content".
- "**what we're looking at**" / "**what do we see here**" as the transition into chart discussion.
- "**I'll get to that in a little bit**" — his standard forward reference, used constantly.
- "**that's the update for today**".

### 3.2 Dutch-English speech markers

He is a Dutch native speaker and the transcripts carry consistent, identifiable non-native patterns.

- **Adverb invention**: "quite fastly", "relatively fastly", "surely" used where "certainly" is meant.
- **"argument" used as a verb**: "you could argument that", "I'm argumenting what would I do".
- **"induce/inducing" used for "imply"**: "inducing that we confirm that we continue the bullish thesis".
- **Preposition and article slips**: "in the markets" where English would drop the article; "I'm into a position"; "on the short term" for "in the short term".
- **"the exact same"** as a fixed phrase, very frequent.
- **Comparative overreach**: "way more", "way bigger", "way faster", "by a mile", "substantially" — "substantially" is possibly his single most overused adverb.
- **Hedge stacking**: "most likely", "very likely", "probably", "relatively", "kind of", "somewhat" — often two or three in one clause. "it is very likely that we're probably going to..."
- **Filler**: "um" and "well" at high density; "to be fair", "to be honest", "honestly", "actually" (very high frequency), "I would say".
- Speech-to-text artefacts reveal his accent: "Fe chain" for VeChain, "eat"/"each" for ETH, "Warhole"/"Warmall"/"War more" for Wormhole, "Phantom" for Fantom, "kadano" for Cardano, "salana" for Solana, "Peppa" for PEPE, "toao" for TAO, "sculps" for scalps, "hatching" for hedging, "AOE"/"AOM"/"API" for edge/AUM/APY, "banmark"/"bare market" for bear market, "offing"/"Hing"/"halfing" for halving, "bips"/"bibs" for basis points, "microeconomics" consistently misused where he means **macro**economics.

### 3.3 Metaphors and recurring images

- **"the station has passed"** — for an asset that has already run and should not be chased (vA3kj9nEGxE, and repeated).
- **"the pendulum"** — for risk-on/risk-off rotation (3mLkDTIA484, a0TRP7J4oDE).
- **"the vacuum"** — the policy window (f8mkENRj98U).
- **"bear market PTSD"** — his coinage for the reflex to expect every correction to continue (R84mPBSVp4A, 9I3U0kjHBiU).
- **"the banana zone"** (borrowed from Raoul Pal), **"going bananas"**, "going absolutely wild", "going off a cliff", "ghost town", "the calm before the storm".
- **"Bitcoin is sneezing and then altcoins are moving downward"** (hX81r-U0N0Q).
- **"balls of steel"** / "if you have some actual balls to get into the markets" — for tolerating drawdown (cXhe_aSnsk8, bOUV1MaoIrc).
- **"don't chase the candles"** / "chasing green candles".
- **"nothing burger"** for a non-event data print (R84mPBSVp4A).
- **"clown world"** for Fed policy (f8mkENRj98U).
- Wall Street Cheat Sheet emotional stages — capitulation, anger, **depression** — used as a literal location marker: "altcoins are currently at depression" (hX81r-U0N0Q, bOUV1MaoIrc).

### 3.4 Emotional register

Notably flat compared to the genre. He does not shout. The affect shows up in dry, self-deprecating asides rather than hype.

- Deadpan about pain: "the altcoin portfolio is still a pain in the ass" (6RPg59KbR0Y); Celestia's 16 red monthly candles are "disgusting behavior, price behavior".
- Gallows humour about losses: "It was great having a summer eating hamburgers, noodles and rice" (cXhe_aSnsk8).
- Self-deprecation about ageing: "the only thing that has changed is I'm turning gray and that's maybe because I'm completely bored" (KLTfFrpeerI); "crypto can turn you quite fast into a boomer and into a very old person" (uMy0nCZkrus).
- Undercuts his own seriousness: "I'm a huge fan of Cardano... I've almost gotten a Cardano tattoo — no, I'm kidding" (O-1c8-TV9Io).
- Rare genuine strain: "It's the biggest challenge of my lifetime I feel like" (OxCUH9e2wvw).
- Audible physical tics captured in the 2025-26 transcripts: repeated `[snorts]`, `[clears throat]` — consistent with the medication/skin issues he discusses (6RPg59KbR0Y, OxCUH9e2wvw, X_yH-OpUgqM).
- Mild profanity only: "pain in the ass", "balls". No heavy swearing in this batch.

### 3.5 Rhetorical habits

- **Binary scenario framing** is his default structure: "there are two scenarios", "the first one is... the other case is...". Almost every market video ends in an explicit fork with triggers.
- **Conditional invalidation levels** are given as a matter of course: "as long as X holds", "if we lose Y then Z".
- **Teaching through worked numbers** — the ETH derisking example, the TVL ratios, the rank-based valuation, the gold sigma calculation.
- **Repetition as pedagogy**: "I keep on repeating that...", "I've said it a few times already", "as I said over and over in the videos".
- **The mission phrase**, near-verbatim across years: "your journey towards a financial stable future" / "financial freedom" / "protecting your purchasing power".
- **Clickbait titles inverted in the body.** Two clear cases: "The Next Bitcoin Crash Will Be Worse Than You Expect (2026)" argues 2026 will *not* be bearish (Ay2QiMJrEpc); "Being In Altcoins Is STUPID!" argues it is not stupid (GYLJbFJH7yw); "Bitcoin Retest at $15K?" rejects $15K (B4dHd4G57yg). This is a deliberate, repeated pattern worth flagging.

## 4. 外部視角（他怎麼被評價、他怎麼回應）

The batch contains unusually rich material here because he quotes his critics directly rather than paraphrasing them.

### 4.1 Criticisms he quotes verbatim

- **"You're always wrong."** — "hey but Michael, you went all into altcoins and you're down 70%, what is your plan then... 'you're always wrong', that's also a comment that we're getting quite often" (uMy0nCZkrus).
- **"He's a complete moron."** — "I will probably receive a lot of comments again saying, 'Why do you follow Michael? He's a complete moron.'" (6RPg59KbR0Y)
- **Mocked in real time for a specific call**: on Chainlink, "yes I said between $6 to $8 is an opportunity of a lifetime — people are making fun of me right now as the price is at five bucks" (wifvrnUwFqQ).
- **Accused of being wrong about the rally**: "I've been attacked on social media by the people that say hey, we are not going to get the run anymore" (TaDV6zbUo9Y).
- **Hate for being bullish**: "I'm getting a lot of hate from people who say that the markets are eager for a retest at 15K" (UMfvHRxOTwk).
- **A specific trade second-guessed**: "I saw some reflection on people that were saying, 'you've sold Omni at a big loss and you should have been keeping the position'" (GYLJbFJH7yw).
- **Portfolio authenticity challenged**: viewers asked whether the public portfolio is fictional (9I3U0kjHBiU).
- **Comments on his appearance and health** — frequent enough that he pre-empts them twice: "if you are going to comment about my skin, you don't need to" (OxCUH9e2wvw); "I've quit my medication which means that my skin is recovering... if you are looking for any comment on my eyes" (mXdek-CLUCw). Also comments asking whether he gets enough sleep (uMy0nCZkrus).

### 4.2 How he responds

Four distinct modes, in rough order of frequency:

1. **Reframe from accuracy to profitability.** "I'd like to be wrong but make money anyways... it's not about being wrong or right, it's about making consistent amount of money" — immediately followed by the concession "which I've not been making in the past five months as we were too early" (uMy0nCZkrus). Also: "I don't know any trader that is right 100% of the time" (9I3U0kjHBiU).
2. **Concede partially, then hold the position anyway.** On being called a moron: "Yeah, maybe he is. Maybe he's not." ## 7. 矛盾與演化

### 7.1 Positions he has genuinely reversed

- **The four-year/halving cycle.** Used its arithmetic in detail in 2023 (UMfvHRxOTwk, Or5MHB3zgnI) — "10 months prior to the halving is the time when you buy altcoins" — then declared it dead by 2025 ("that's why I believe the four-year cycle is dead", bOUV1MaoIrc; "delete everything surrounding the four-year cycle", YB83E3sJfOQ). He flags the shift himself, so this reads as documented evolution rather than concealed reversal. What he does **not** address is that his halving-based 2023 calls and his liquidity-based 2025 calls were both wrong on magnitude.
- **Old coins vs new coins.** In cXhe_aSnsk8 he swaps out Chainlink and other older assets for newer ones because "this is going to be the final easy cycle" and new tech will outperform. In GSpyPBVq960 he argues the opposite — that projects building since 2017 with good treasury management will survive, and that the "invest only in newer coins" thesis is overstated. The pro-old-coin argument appears in the *sponsored* VeChain video. He then returns to Chainlink as a buy in 2026 (X_yH-OpUgqM) without noting he had sold it.
- **Small caps vs blue chips.** 2024: fully allocated to eight high-risk alts with no BTC or ETH, plus DEX-traded micro caps. 2026: "you don't need to go into smaller altcoins. You're better off by just buying the large caps or the blue chips" (X_yH-OpUgqM). The reversal follows a ~70% drawdown and is not labelled as a lesson learned.
- **Leverage.** Tells retail "You should not be trading with leverage" (X_yH-OpUgqM) and "-gZQXf5BgTk" ("don't use leverage"), while in the same period promoting OKX spot margin and describing his own use of leverage for delta-neutral and volatility strategies — "leverage can become a tool instead of a risk" (Ay2QiMJrEpc). The distinction he draws (hedging vs directional) is defensible but is not always made clear to the audience being sold the product.
- **Yields as a signal.** The core model says falling yields are required for ETH to perform. After the Trump election he re-frames rising yields as bullish — "the yields going up is not a sign of weakness, it's a sign of strength" (uMy0nCZkrus). The model is adjusted to fit the tape without acknowledgement.

### 7.2 Standing internal tensions

- **The perpetually deferred crash.** He has forecast a generational crisis repeatedly since 2023 — "we are cruising towards a very big crisis" (cXWpXBdikwk), a 2008-comparable depression in 2023 (f8mkENRj98U), "the biggest crisis and the biggest crash of a lifetime" (Ay2QiMJrEpc), "the biggest ever" (Hg-LLWEWisI) — while always placing it *after* the next rally. In Hg-LLWEWisI: "Bitcoin is the final needle and then it's going to crash big time." The crash thesis is structurally unfalsifiable as stated, because the timing recedes with each video.
- **Fallibility acknowledged, then certainty asserted.** The pattern is explicit: "I've been wrong many times in my life, but given the data that I'm seeing, I'm sure that a bearish market is not on our cards" (Ay2QiMJrEpc). Also "if you want more arguments, those are not there. This is it." (Or5MHB3zgnI). The humility is real but does not translate into position sizing or confidence calibration.
- **"It's not about being right, it's about making money"** (uMy0nCZkrus) — deployed as a defence against wrong calls, but in the same breath he concedes he was not making money either. The reframe does not survive its own sentence.
- **The exogenous-distortion pattern.** Disappointing outcomes are consistently attributed to outside interference rather than to model error: the 2021 cycle "should have gone to 150-200K" but for FTX/Alameda suppression (KLTfFrpeerI, uMy0nCZkrus, UMfvHRxOTwk, QTFB6klElSU); the COVID crash was distorted by "the BitMEX engine filling"; the Oct/Nov 2025 crash was "not natural at all", "someone was just pressing sell" (OxCUH9e2wvw). This is used to rescue the diminishing-returns rebuttal that produced the 300-400K target. Sometimes plausible; as a *pattern* it insulates the framework from disconfirmation.
- **Cash discipline he teaches but does not follow.** He teaches that cash is essential for flexibility and that a non-zero probability of correction always exists (vA3kj9nEGxE). Yet: "I don't have liquidity left so I stick to the play" (6QjyCf9BzD4, said twice), and he was 100% allocated to altcoins with no BTC or ETH through the 2024-25 drawdown (cXhe_aSnsk8). He acknowledges being "always too early" but does not connect it to the absence of dry powder.
- **The "too early" flaw is named but never structurally fixed.** He identifies it in 2024 (cXhe_aSnsk8) and it recurs through 2025-26. His mitigation is psychological (patience, price-based rebalancing) rather than procedural (staged entries, invalidation discipline). He states his preference explicitly: "I'd rather be positioned into altcoins earlier than I should have been" (KLTfFrpeerI) — so it is a deliberate trade-off, not purely an error, but the cost is repeatedly borne.
- **Stated invalidation levels are not honoured.** ETH/BTC 0.05 was given as the condition for staying long (ucjDDYaSyQQ); it broke and he stayed long. This is the clearest gap between his stated process and his behaviour.
- **Empathy vs dismissiveness toward his audience.** He is unusually candid about the pain of holding ("You are one of the warriors", 3mLkDTIA484) and then tells losing retail investors to "stop crying" if they still have a job (-gZQXf5BgTk). He calls those who doubt altcoin returns "foolish" (YB83E3sJfOQ) while elsewhere validating exactly that doubt (GYLJbFJH7yw).
- **Elections/politics.** Insists elections are overrated relative to labour-market data (a0TRP7J4oDE, KLTfFrpeerI) — a defensible and well-argued position — then produces a full Trump-catalyst video treating the election as the market's key breaker (uMy0nCZkrus). He also separates his ethics from his positioning: "from an ethical standpoint I do not believe it's great for the world, but we'll see."

### 7.3 Blind spots he does not engage with

- **No systematic post-mortem of the 2025 alt basket.** He documents the drawdown transparently and repeatedly, but never revisits *why* the LINK/SEI/PEAQ/Wormhole thesis failed as a thesis. The explanation offered is always macro ("macro pushed price down"), which is the same variable he uses to justify staying long — so it cannot function as a disconfirmation.
- **The TVL/market-cap anchor never gets stress-tested.** Renzo, Omni and Rocket Pool were all justified by extreme TVL ratios and all fell heavily. The ratio's failure as a predictor is not addressed.
- **Sponsor conflict is disclosed but never analysed.** He discloses partnerships, but does not discuss whether promoting exchanges to an audience he simultaneously tells not to over-trade creates a tension.
- **Sample size.** The Chinese-yuan correlation rests on three or four observations, and the gold-ETH rotation on a similar count. He presents both as reliable mechanisms rather than as low-N regularities. He applies rigorous scepticism to *other* people's cycle charts (Hg-LLWEWisI) but not to his own composite indicators.
- **The "final easy cycle" claim** (cXhe_aSnsk8, uMy0nCZkrus — "the final easy optimal financial decision you could make") is urgency-generating marketing language that sits awkwardly next to his own longer-cycle thesis, and it is never reconciled.

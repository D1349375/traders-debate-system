## 2. 風控：教 1-3% → 單日數萬~數十萬虧損（帳戶規模膨脹辯護 vs 情緒證據並存）

- **早期教學基準**：多支影片一致教學「每筆風險1-2%（有時1-3%），同一天原則上只交易一筆」(`nQj01ZbJ6S0`, 2023-02-16；`vlnNPFu4rEQ`；`7C1FxfjIC54`, 2023-02-05："risk 1% per trade")；`RkMBoqbeUq8`(Boot Camp Day13)：「risk one to three percent of your account...for risk management one to three percent A DAY」。
- **後期實際規模**：

  - `av3QLilFxek`(2025-11-20)：單日-$151,780，罕見自我批評「I think I need to size down. It's definitely been affecting my mental」。
  - `NLRBtK0jnxQ`(2025-03-15)：主動聲明「I've been so risk off due to crypto. But now I'm looking to be more risk」，同日即虧$45,000。
  - `KxBRLErkel0`(2025-05-26)：推出「aggressive scalping strategy」，強調"不是取代"原策略而是疊加——此防禦性措辭本身顯示他預期會被質疑風控前後不一。

## 5. 策略簡化：2023 五件套 → 2025 棄用 order block/breaker → 「strategies don't expire」辯護

- **2023 完整五件套教學**（Boot Camp系列反覆逐日教學）：liquidity sweep、break of structure、order block、fair value gap、equilibrium／breaker block，五者並用作為策略地基。引用：「liquidity sweep break of structure order block fair value gap equilibrium and Order blocks that is it...there's no extra Secret Sauce」(`AUUZ9Vk6p5E`, 2022-12-12)。
- **2025 明確棄用**：
  - `wzq2AMsoJKY`：「I no longer use order blocks. I no longer use breaker blocks...The only continuation confluences that you guys need...are equilibrium and fair value gaps」。
  - `BMyeYQIj_vU`(2025-09-17)：「I completely removed order blocks and breaker blocks...they've pretty much become useless to me over the past year of trading...as people get better and better at stuff...they tend to simplify simplify simplify」。
  - `_rUMSqAtppo`：「Order blocks. When was the last time that you heard me use an order block? Order blocks are completely useless, respectfully.」
- **辯護話術（未承認是推翻，而是「進化」）**：
  - `kU9qxNu4Sfo`(2026-02-24)：「if you guys think that the boot camp is like the holy grail...I'm a much better trader now」。
  - `7a8tAGkVJac`(2025-10-30)：直接承認「the boot camp is dated, okay? I do not trade the same way...There's some days that I'm actually willing to trade on now that previously in the boot camp I said not to trade on」——他自己點名這是YouTube教學內容的通病。
  - `ftWUenk3xrk`(2024-05-16)：在被質疑策略搖擺時辯護「strategies don't expire...just because I made a YouTube video on it doesn't mean I'm going to use it every day」。
  - `shXF1M3fz4Q`：「the way that I'm trading now is so different compared to two years ago...you have to have recency bias with pretty much all of this」，並主動要求觀眾不要用舊教材評斷現在的交易決策。
- **相關次要搖擺**：SMT背離指數選擇（早期偏好lagging index → 2025-04明確說「changed that around...two and a half to three months ago」改favoring non-lagging/leading index，`Qr6nzziTQPU`, `Q3c5E653bi8`）；order block曾在2023年前後被稱為策略「第一層安全網」(`T-Bo-9GDi3s`)，但同年稍晚(`-HLOFkJtBmA`, 2023-05-19)又說「I don't really like using order blocks...they're cuter better name」（fair value gap）——理由帶主觀審美而非技術論證。

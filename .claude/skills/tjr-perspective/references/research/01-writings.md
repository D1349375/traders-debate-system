## A. 市場結構 / 流動性核心模型

### A1. Draws on Liquidity / 流動性磁鐵框架（最核心、最高頻模型）
一句話：市場永遠在尋找「draw on liquidity」（等高/等低點、前高前低、session highs/lows、data highs/lows）或「imbalance/fair value gap」去填補，找方向的第一步永遠是先標出這些磁鐵點。
- 「what do price always going to do it's going to seek out draws on liquidity or it's going to seek out imbalances」(`9kFa4AQwDNg`, 2024-08-26)
- 「Where is the next draw on liquidity? All the way over here, these monthly highs.」(`-rYGugTIsbU`, 2023-07-17)
- 「it determines where the market is drawn towards... it's like a magnet」（電影 Robots 巨大磁鐵比喻）(`5MJbi51w8tk`, 2024-02-20)
- 「price moves off of draws on liquidity and imbalance price. That's how price is able to move.」(`FBW9r6PeRGU`, 2025-08-01)
- 「Liquidity is step one of our strategy...price has the opportunity to fill orders above highs and below lows.」(`4sRDnVmLcMk`, 2026-01-09)
- 跨批出現範圍：**全部16批**（Batch01-16），是唯一在每一批研究筆記中都被列為核心/最高頻模型的框架，可視為 TJR 整套策略的地基。

### A2. 高時間框架宰制低時間框架 (Higher Time Frame Holds Higher Power / Top-down HTF alignment)
一句話：日內方向判斷必須先服從週線/日線/4H/1H的結構與流動性目標，低時間框架的反向訊號多半只是高時間框架的回撤(retrace)而非反轉。
- 「like you don't want to be trading down when you have all those high time frames saying up」(`-rYGugTIsbU`, 2023-07-17)
- 「the best losses are when the higher time frames go against your bias」(`bQwAlwVuPbI`, 2023-10-27)
- 「higher time frame holds higher power for the most part or usually usually all the time」(`P66EsTcHqRU`, 2024-01-19)
- 「higher time frames hold higher power within the market」(`RQSVIKddFHI`, Boot Camp Day22)
- 「on the daily if we get a 4-H hour break of structure to the upside odds are it's a daily retracement」(`bQwAlwVuPbI`, 2023-10-27)
- 「higher time frames hold higher power...we need to be understanding the high time frames in order to understand where price wants to go」(`Z2O1BEtw4Lc`, 2025-09-12)
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch01(隱含)、02、03、04、05、06、07、08、09、11、13(隱含)、14、15(隱含)、16 —— 幾乎每一批都出現，是與 A1 並列最頻繁的核心模型。

### A3. 價格是碎形 (Price is Fractal)
一句話：同一套 confluence（liquidity sweep、BOS、FVG、equilibrium、order block）在任何時間框架都適用，規模不同但邏輯相同，可用於把 HTF 偏見 scale down 到 LTF 執行。
- 「price is fractal all of the Confluence confluences that I teach happen on literally every single time frame」(`VRGing9SiA8`, 無日期)
- 「Price is fractal... the reason that price is moving on the daily time frame is the same reason why price moves a certain way on the 1 hour time frame or the one minute time frame」(`FBW9r6PeRGU`, 2025-08-01)
- 「price moves the same way on every single time frame」(`NZqRbc_9u_I`, 2025-02-12；`kl_aMLMISdg`, 2025-09-29)
- PM session 是 AM session 策略的「縮時版」：「it's exactly like my strategy we're just using lower time frames」(`UR6vM7nPBeo`, 無日期)
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch02、03、09、11、15。

### A4. 市場如賭場／莊家對手模型 (House-vs-retail liquidity model)
一句話：市場本質是機構/做市商為了讓大單成交而「操縱」高低點，散戶止損與追價單即是被獵殺的流動性；交易者要站在莊家/機構那一邊，而非散戶那一邊。
- 「the market is like a casino okay you guys get in there and you spin the wheel and you guys gamble what do we know about casinos The House Always Wins... the only way for us to win as traders is to trade with the market」(`cvFXeyikZoQ`, 2025-01-10)
- 「Liquidity is resting orders... The market needs orders to be filled in order to push the market in the direction that it wants to go.」(`crMqxB_nHhk`, 2026-01-04)
- 「we want to be part of the... market makers... not the crowd」(`GJbC6nO3oHQ`)
- 「liquidity isn't something that Banks hedge funds and institutions just like get it's something that they have to induce sweep liquidity in order for their orders to get filled」(`XxJAkUfbs0k`)
- 「Liquidity is resting orders... why do we use it because we know that that is where reversals are going to happen」(`_Fkofoggjv4`)
- 種族化時段擬人：把亞洲盤/倫敦盤/紐約盤擬人成會互相「操縱」彼此高低點的角色（見表達DNA節，內容具冒犯性）(`AGmAVyAuBE0`)
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch01(隱含反指標段)、03、06、08、10、14。

### A5. SMT背離 / 跨市場驗證 (SMT Divergence, leading vs lagging index)
一句話：用兩個高相關指數（ES/NASDAQ）互相印證方向——若一個創新高/新低而另一個沒有，代表操縱尚未明朗，應交易「較弱」的那一方以追隨「領先指數」。**注意：他對「該交易領先還是落後指數」的立場有明確的時間演變（見05檔矛盾/演變記錄）**。
- 「NASDAQ is the leading index on that bearish SMT... we're going to favor that one that's pushing in the direction that we want it to go in, not the lagging one」(`4eT-GMJ7ypc`, 2025-09-15)
- 「SMT divergence isn't going to tell me like one thing or another... It just strengthens our bias」(`TNyTn30CPVg`)——強調 SMT 只是加強偏見，不能單獨觸發進場
- 「I've been seeing that the leading index on SMT divergences has just been providing better volume」(`Q3c5E653bi8`, 近期)
- 早期教學明確坦言不確定術語全稱：「我根本不知道 SMT 縮寫的正確全名」(`7dTQA0t8SH0`, 2026-01-11)
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch02、05、07、09、10、11、13、14、16。

### A6. Equilibrium / 折溢價 (Premium/Discount)
一句話：從最近一次擺動高低點量出50%中線作為「均衡點」，機構在折價區買、溢價區賣；equilibrium 屬於「延續性confluence」而非「反轉confluence」。標記equilibrium永遠只取「最近一次」的高低點，舊的作廢。
- 「smart money never buys in a premium smart money never never shorts in a premium they always buy and they was short in a discount」(`-E7plUbSJUk`, 2023-06-23)
- 「when are they buying? in a discount...when are they selling? at a premium...this is our 50 mark」(`lyXKaPy-1SU`, 2022-11-11)
- 「it measures premium in discount... because of this it's a continuation Confluence」(`fl9ofZ4Rtb0`, 2024-03-07)
- equilibrium 只能用「最近一次」高低點畫，舊高低點作廢（強調語氣最激動的細節之一）(`joe_XTCn5Bs`；`wzq2AMsoJKY`；`n97MKj6wGFk`, 2024-11-19)
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch05、09(隱含equilibrium是持續留用的confluence之一)、12、15、16；早在2022年底(`lyXKaPy-1SU`)已系統化教授。

### A7. 低阻力 vs 高阻力流動性 (Low/High Resistance Liquidity)
一句話：把鄰近多個 session 高低點視為一組，判斷這組流動性是「強力吸引目標」（低阻力，尚未被觸及）還是「已被掃過的弱目標」（高阻力）。市場傾向一次掃過整組堆疊高低點，因為機構可以藉此填更多訂單。
- 「Group them all together. What is it? Low, resistance, liquidity」(`FQLzhkiUVlw`, 2025-05-09)
- 「it doesn't really make too much sense. It would ideally want to take out all of them」(`KxBRLErkel0`, 2026-01-27)
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch02、04、08、12、13、16。

### A8. Seek and Destroy（獵殺回踩模型）
一句話：約 10:30-11:00（或早盤稍後）期間，市場會先把早盤看似正確的順勢單止損在盈虧平衡點或小虧洗出去，再展開真正的大行情；建議晚一點進場或不因這種洗盤就否定原偏見。
- 「more often than not before price creates and completes the full High time frame move... it will seek and destroy these entry points」(`p5CKu0FNbyg`)
- 「remember that Seek and Destroy method... stops the ideal am session trade out at break even or a little bit lower and then continues」(`-7-oZkdB4Vs`, 2024-11-06；`YUSmh2LtHRo`, 2024-09-12)
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch06(隱含"Seek and Destroy"現象)、10、12、14、16。他自陳這是自己觀察並命名的獨立模型（推斷：可能呼應 ICT 的 stop hunt 概念）。

### A9. Time Theory / 操縱-進場時段模型 (Manipulation window → Macro entry window)
一句話：紐約盤 9:30-9:50 為「操縱期」，9:50-10:10 為「進場期(macro)」，10:30 後若無單則收工；同樣邏輯延伸至 PM session（13:50-14:10 macro）。但他明確表態不完全遵循 ICT 機械化的 Kill Zone/macro 教條，強調是憑經驗抓出的個人化時窗。
- 「manipulation time frame, which is from 9:30 to 9:50...then from 9:50 really till like 10:10...that's our typical entry」(`L4xz2o23aPQ`, 2026-01-12)
- 「I don't necessarily follow ict's Kill Zone to a [__] T I don't necessarily follow all the macro this and that」(`r-7-gWZsnZc`, 無日期)
- 一天只用「9:50 macro之後」的時段作為主要進場窗口，開盤前1.5小時為最佳交易時段(`W7izYiBHWW4`, 2024-03-05)
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch02、07(隱含kill zone)、12、14、16。

### A10. 時段接力模型 (Session Relay：London manipulation → New York continuation)
一句話：若高時間框架流動性掃蕩發生在盤前/前一session，紐約開盤後仍須等待一次「低時間框架操縱」才可進場，因為「新資金入場需要自己的操縱」，不能直接沿用舊的掃蕩當作進場依據。
- 「if the liquidity sweep happens during pre-market... we are going to wait for a five-minute manipulation... because new money is coming into the market.」(`-Tl6Dr5seeE`, 2025-10-23；`8QDDxwUV1KM`, 2025-10-06；`zSgXNr2-1Vw`)
- 早期自創術語「London fake out」：他聲稱在認識 ICT 之前，自己發現流動性清掃現象時就取了這個名字：「I called liquidity sweeps before I [bleep] knew what they were. I called them a London fake out」(`Xq6-oO2n6-U`, Boot Camp Day1, 2023)
- 「London manipulation, New York reversal」為早期(Batch01)自創口語化說法。
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch01、02(隱含)、14。

### A11. 三種每日型態 (Three Daily Profiles) —— 機械式判斷法（獨立且系統化的框架）
一句話：他明確教學「過去一個session只有三種可能走法」：(1) 前一session盤整→新session操縱+反轉；(2) 前一session操縱但未反轉→新session反轉；(3) 前一session操縱+已反轉→新session延續。這是他對「如何找daily bias」問題給出的最完整、最機械化的公開教學。
- 「there's literally only three ways that the past session can move」(`e_FV0Q14k8E`, 2025-06-30)
- 跨批出現範圍：主要見於Batch13（獨立完整教學），與A1/A2/A9/A10共同構成他的「daily bias」推導骨架。

### A12. 五件套 / 三層確認堆疊進場模型 (Liquidity Sweep → BOS/Inverse FVG → FVG/OB/Equilibrium)
一句話：他反覆用三段式描述進場邏輯——「reversal confluence」(流動性掃蕩) → 「confirmation confluence」(break of structure / inverse FVG) → 「continuation confluence」(FVG/equilibrium/order block)，缺一則視為低機率不進場。也稱為「拼圖式執行框架 (Putting the Pieces Together)」：五大 building block（liquidity sweep、BOS、order block、FVG、equilibrium）組合越多越安全。
- 「liquidity...that's the reversal confluence...break of structure...I call a confirmation confluence...fair value gaps [are] a continuation confluence」(`4sRDnVmLcMk`, 2026-01-09)
- 「a liquidity sweep break of structure and then order block or for Value Gap entry so that's what we waited for」(`It4_gJjSOyY`, 2023-07-11)
- 「liquidity sweep breaker structure order block liquidity sweep breakup structure for Value Gap...along with the confirmation of price being in a discount」(`ESsy0uoFAz4`, 2023-06-25)
- 「liquidity sweep break of structure fair value Gap equilibrium and Order blocks that is it... there's no extra Secret Sauce」(`AUUZ9Vk6p5E`, 2022-12-12)
- 跨批出現範圍：**幾乎全部16批**，是他所有交易錄影/日內偏見教學的執行骨架。

### A13. Inverse FVG 等同於 Break of Structure
一句話：inverse fair value gap（失衡缺口被逆向突破）在功能上等同於 break of structure，兩者可互為替代確認訊號，「哪個先出現就用哪個」；他也明確反對「一定要等FVG被填補回來」的教條，認為FVG失效本身就可以是進場理由。
- 「inverse fair value gaps are confirmation confluences and are equal to break of structure...I just use whichever comes first」(`xcDOMKaOIsk`, 無日期)
- 「I'm not necessarily a huge fan of using inverse fair value gaps like getting tapped back into as like a Confluence... I am a fan of seeing fair value gaps getting invalidated and taking trades based off of that」(`rMkghkG-ZjE`, 無日期)
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch15；概念延伸見於多支交易錄影中反覆使用 inverse FVG 作為 5分鐘/1分鐘確認訊號（04、05、06、09、13、14）。

### A14. 簡化主義／砍掉多餘Confluence (Simplification = Mastery)
一句話：策略隨年資演化，明確從「多confluence並用（liquidity sweep+BOS+order block+breaker block+FVG+equilibrium）」瘦身為「只用 liquidity sweep+BOS+equilibrium/FVG」，認為 order block/breaker block 已成「多餘產物」；他將此描述為「越練越簡化」的自然進化而非推翻舊教學。
- 「I no longer use order blocks. I no longer use breaker blocks... The only continuation confluences that you guys need... are equilibrium and fair value gaps」(`wzq2AMsoJKY`, 無日期)
- 「I completely removed order blocks and breaker blocks... they've pretty much become useless to me over the past year of trading... as people get better and better at stuff... they tend to simplify simplify simplify.」(`BMyeYQIj_vU`, 2025-09-17)
- 「Order blocks. When was the last time that you heard me use an order block? Order blocks are completely useless, respectfully.」(`_rUMSqAtppo`, 無日期)
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch05、08、10(隱含反覆論證SMC不會過時)、14、16——同時也是「矛盾/演變」章節的重點案例（2023年 Boot Camp 曾把 order block/breaker block 列為必修 building block）。

### A15. 反技術指標 / 反傳統支撐阻力 / 純Price Action信仰
一句話：完全拒絕使用RSI、MACD、均線交叉、Fibonacci circle/wedge/pitchfork、趨勢線、支撐阻力等傳統技術分析工具，主張這些是「retail trap／視覺垃圾」，唯一有效的是流動性/結構/失衡類的SMC concepts。
- 「indicators are like the Holy Grail I thought...guys it doesn't work」(`-Myd0b6xCh4`)
- 「it bounced off a floor so I'm going to buy... that makes no sense to me... that's guessing」(`Lxnu7InGhGE`, 2023-06-02)
- 「trend lines...they are so vague...everybody's trend line is different」(`5dPZEeDqpbM`, 2023-09-21)
- 「If you're putting one of these [Fibonacci circle/wedge/pitchfork/ABCD/cipher] on your charts, just give up, bro... genuinely give up」(`eEg0_zc8Hxg`, 2026-07-06)——**同一批影片中他同時承認自己使用 Fibonacci retracement 與 Gann box 作equilibrium/retracement工具，構成明顯的定義雙重標準**（詳見05檔矛盾記錄）。
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch01、03、08、09、12、16。

### A16. 極簡主義教學哲學：一個策略、一個導師 (KISS)
一句話：反對同時學多個策略/多個導師、反對過度分析(「overlearn and underpractice」)；只交易一個商品、一個session、一套step-by-step流程。
- 「you guys are trading a different strategy every [expletive] day because it's not systemized」(`QI1ihQiH-F8`)
- 「have one strategy, have one mentor... keep it simple stupid, K-I-S-S」(`2Yjg-gqj33A`, 2024-02-14)
- 「stop over complicating stuff... be a pro at one」(`LG_IbfjbaOo`, 2023-07-10)
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch01、02(隱含"basics>complexity")、05、13。

## B. 交易哲學 / 心理模型

### B1. 機率優先於預測 (Probability over Prediction)
一句話：交易不是「預測每一步」，而是執行一套長期勝率>敗率的系統，讓機率自然發揮作用；虧損是策略內建成本，不代表策略失效。
- 「everything that you're doing Within These markets is based off of probability... The House Always Wins unless you have an edge within the market」(`yK2tYsDZlj4`)
- 「there's no strategy that has a 100% win rate. So when you lose, that should be accepted.」(`sBmcenZDwk4`)
- 「Your strategy is an indication of a higher probability of one thing happening over another... you don't need to know what will happen next to make money.」(`88tyzO9CxGA`, 2026-07-13)
- 「Trading isn't about making money... our main goal within trading is being able to predict where price wants to go with a high probability.」(`xRuMUvW3T7Q`, 2025-07-10)
- 「trading is getting onto the markets every single day over a long period of time and accurately predicting where price wants to go with a high probability」(`8PYgFVB0GHE`, 2026-06-05)
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch02、03、04、06、07、08、09、11、12、13——是他心理學/教學內容中出現頻率最高的口頭禪級信念之一。

### B2. 不要嫁給你的偏見 / 持續適應模型 (Never Marry Your Bias / Adapt-React-Readapt)
一句話：即使開盤前設定的方向偏見很有信心，一旦市場（尤其5分鐘order flow）持續反向確認，就必須放棄原偏見、順勢交易；他反覆承認這是自己最大的交易錯誤來源。
- 「I'm every time I lose a trade bro I'm marrying bias I'm marrying my my whatever my bias and I'm not reading what the market is telling me to do」(`MzJK2-NDOhk`, 2025-03-10)
- 「my bias will and has to change based on 5 minute order flow... you guys need to stop marrying marrying your bias」(`-RAJEWMl-F0`, 2025-02-04)
- 「if price tells us to go against it, then we kind of have to. We can't just listen to what we want.」(`3SygD-YgZuQ`)
- 「Market doesn't give a shit about my bias and we have to adapt react readapt」(`ojAgfBBJ-uU`, 2025-03-04)
- 「more often than not my initial bias at 9:00 a.m. is wrong bro more often than not... but I'm really good at adapting and reacting to what the market gives me」(`ojAgfBBJ-uU`, 2025-03-04)
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch02、04、06、10——同時也是他反覆自我檢討虧損時最常引用的自省語彙（「married my bias」已幾乎變成他自創的固定術語，貶義自嘲）。

### B3. 三支柱框架：策略 / 風控 / 心理 (Strategy + Risk Management + Psychology)
一句話：交易獲利需三項技能同時具備，但策略只是「最簡單的一環」（甚至只佔5%），心理與風控才是真正的分水嶺與長期護城河。
- 「profitable Trader we need three trads okay strategy number one psychology number two risk management number three」(`a5PLl1uM7CQ`, 2025-03-18)
- 「Strategy for most traders is the least of their worries... The most difficult skills to be able to build... are the two that I covered before this [psychology, risk management].」(`fmLqRceJaYU`, 2025-10-11)
- 「There are three skill sets that we need to learn as traders... strategy, good risk management, and then your psychology... psychology literally just means sticking to both of these things」(`hBnD6T1M4w8`, 2026-01-16)
- 「psychology for sure, strategy is like 5% of trading」(`ik3aIvZ4NrU`, 2023-10-21)
- 「day trading is really only 10 to 15% of day trading, the rest is psychological」(`NK-RsDCp4Lg`, 2022-10-31)
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch03、05、06、08(隱含)、11、13。

### B4. 交易是可習得的技能，不是致富捷徑 (Trading as a Skill, not Get-Rich-Quick)
一句話：把交易類比籃球/建築工人技能——先練基本功，錢是副產品；反覆強調要以「5-10年」尺度思考，需給自己至少2年時間才能轉為穩定獲利。
- 「What makes you think that you can do that in two weeks?...All of the best rewards lie at the end of years and months of hard work」(`8FO-Dal-L7U`, 2025-08-11)
- 「think in 5 to 10 year increments instead of day-to-day increments」(`fdS8SmXLwWA`, 2024-04-17)
- 「day trading is not going to get you rich quick... give me two years」(`AEoCaWFRXHU`, 2025-03-24)
- 「trading isn't about making money it's about predicting where price wants to go every single day」(`MNA9gtZCVqM`, 2024-04-15)
- 「Day trading is not... about making money... The awesome side effect... of gaining that skill set is making money.」(`R62RnJrKH3Q`)
- 籃球類比：「I love comparing trading to basketball because it's super similar... you just have to be insanely good at the fundamentals」(`CPPREA-6ubY`, 2025-04-14)
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch01、04、07、08、11、12、13——與他大量展示豪車/名錶的生活方式內容形成刻意的敘事張力（見05檔矛盾記錄）。

### B5. 金錢是工具，不是目的 (Money as a Tool / Emotional Detachment from Money)
一句話：一旦情感上「需要」某筆錢，交易績效就會受損；成功來自把錢視為達成人生目標的工具，而非目標本身；獲利/虧損金額本身不該引發情緒。
- 「once I learned to detach myself from money and realize that money was just a tool... that was really freaking big in my trading journey」(`IJ6cHg1F-aE`, 2025-03-26)
- 「money is literally a unit of measurement」(`Xq6-oO2n6-U`, Boot Camp Day1)
- 「if you win $20,000 I'm like cool that's just a percentage gain... you cannot act based off money」(`z9p7OThLkr4`)
- 「money always comes second/money always comes last」(`Xq6-oO2n6-U`)
- 延伸至長期資產配置模型：把獲利分配為稅金／再投入新資金帳戶／傳統投資組合(S&P500/國債/高收益儲蓄)／少量犒賞消費。引用：「diversify your Investment Portfolio... real estate stocks indexes crypto commodity cars watches high yield savings bonds treasuries」(`eJLqMkfUHkI`)
- 恐慌時貪婪：長線美股DCA觀（2025關稅崩盤後新增）：「when others are fearful, we should be greedy... I personally am investing very heavily in the S&P 500」(`e4v45zQAX5c`, 2025-04-08)
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch02(恐慌貪婪/DCA)、04、08、09(funded槓桿工具)、10(分散配置)——與其大量炫富內容存在張力（詳見05檔）。

### B6. 絕對自我歸責 / 去受害者化 (Radical Self-Accountability / Anti-Victimhood)
一句話：虧損、錯過交易、人生不順都是自己造成的，不能怪市場、新聞、莊家、prop firm或原生家庭；一旦承認"我是自己現況的成因"，就拿回了改變的主導權。
- 「it's never the Market's fault... it's up to you to figure out why it's moving that way」(`ykm5K4LBRxA`)
- 「You are the reason why you suck at trading」(`z9p7OThLkr4`)
- 「The market owes you absolutely nothing... You just suck at trading.」(`88tyzO9CxGA`, 2026-07-13)
- 「stop blaming the markets... you are the reason why you lost money and you are the reason why you made money」(`OyA49XKIK7w`)
- 「once you realize that you were the actual reason why you failed the account... you also have the power to pass it」(`JY4DK_ZLDqQ`, 2026-03-16)
- 「You suck. Your strategy doesn't... it's not the strategy that is going to help you turn profitable」(`-zyDnjozaMM`, 2025-11-26)
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch03、07、08、10、12——延伸應用到非交易類的人生建議內容。

### B7. 個別交易與自我價值脫鉤 (Detach Identity from Single-Trade Outcomes)
一句話：單一輸贏不代表你是好/壞交易員，只有長期樣本數才算數；每筆交易都是獨立事件，不因「昨天同樣設定贏了」就假設今天也贏。
- 「Does one losing trade mean that you are a bad trader? The answer is no.」(`88tyzO9CxGA`, 2026-07-13)
- 「Every single trade is independent...never assume today wins because yesterday won」（同一框架的延伸應用，見05決策啟發式）
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch07、08、13(隱含情緒中立)。

### B8. 個體化 / 反教條主義 ("Do What Works For You")
一句話：明確主張學生不該原封不動照抄他的策略，應該混搭多位老師的元素、找出對自己有意義的邏輯；反對「複製貼上式」的僵化四步驟策略。
- 「do not use tjrs 123 strategy... create one for yourself... use those tools and those confluences that we give to you guys so you can create your own strategy.」(`7zAy8xbzoNo`, 2023-11-27)
- 「stop trying to force something that doesn't work just because you have some sort of attachment... be your own person within this.」(`_6voYEdi5j4`, Boot Camp Day 7"Individuality")
- 「this is your this is your art okay make it your own every person that does chart work it is their own... no profitable Trader trades the exact same way」(`zAWKsS6kw4Y`)
- 「training is a very very personal gain and once you realize that... that's when you will turn into a great Trader」(`0PRUVOrVVdg`, 2023-08-19)
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch03、06、10、13(併入KISS段落，注意KISS本身談「單一導師」，個體化談「打造自己的畫布」——兩者表面略有張力但他視為互補：先師從一人打基礎，再發展個人風格)。

### B9. 反技術指標之外的「賭徒vs交易員」身分切割
一句話：主張只有「賺錢的才算真正的day trader」，把「day trading is gambling」的批評重新定義為「他們還不夠格被稱為交易員」（推斷：一種自我豁免式話術）。
- 「are they even a day trader at that point if they're not even making money...they're not probable within the market」(`-Myd0b6xCh4`, 2022-12-24)
- 跨批出現範圍：主要見於Batch01；後續批次中他也自稱「a profitable gambler」，模糊了原本想劃清的界線（見05矛盾記錄）。

### B10. 顯化 / 潛意識程式化 (Manifestation & Reprogram Your Subconscious)
一句話：策略早已足夠，真正卡關是「潛意識」；用現在式肯定句寫日記(I am a profitable trader)、把自己當成已達成目標的人來行動；顯化本身沒用，關鍵是它驅動的行為模仿。
- 「The real breakthrough didn't happen by me changing something in my strategy...The biggest adjustment for me was remanaging and rearranging how I thought about trading」(`8FO-Dal-L7U`, 2025-08-11)
- 「your focus becomes your reality」「I am successful」式現在式肯定句書寫法(`YYoghwU43Kw`)
- 「the people who are delusional enough to be able to think, hey, I can do this and this is possible... those are the people that are going to make it」(`FBW9r6PeRGU`)
- 「manifestation... it's all about the actions that you take after... it trains your subconscious mind to start thinking that you actually are that type of person and then... you start taking the actions that that type of person would do.」(`3hIOK0xp-tM`, 2023-12-26)——並提出「反向思考法」：先列出「最爛交易員會做什麼」再全部反過來做。
- 「You have to trade as if you are a profitable Trader even if you're not」(`G7eq6U8e3yw`, 2023-08-25)
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch01、03、09、14。

### B11. 反常人格 / 擁抱怪異特質 (Embrace-the-Tweak Identity Model)
一句話：把ADD/ADHD/自閉譜系特質重新框架為「超能力」，反權威、不服從常規（"tweaker"）才能致富；用來合理化他叛逆/口無遮攔的公眾形象，反過來成為行銷資產。
- 「Being retarded is probably the biggest blessing that you could have been given.」(`BKEi-Jy5FbY`, 2026-04-23)
- 跨批出現範圍：主要見於Batch07，屬於他個人品牌敘事中較獨特、未在其他批次重複出現的深層信念（推斷為其勵志人設的核心組件）。

### B12. Delusional Confidence 作為成功前提
一句話：自我催眠/自欺式的極端自信（"我一定能做到"）被他視為成功者與失敗者的分水嶺之一，即使這種信念在當下缺乏客觀證據支持。
- 「the people who are delusional enough to be able to think, hey, I can do this and this is possible... those are the people that are going to make it」(`FBW9r6PeRGU`)
- 跨批出現範圍：Batch09（與B10顯化模型高度重疊，視為同一心理機制的不同表述）。

一句話：定位資金挑戰帳戶（funded account）為「新手放大資金能力的核心工具」，主張沒有資本不可恥，這是低資本交易者「合法擴大部位規模」的路徑；同時也存在對prop firm產業「設計來讓你輸」的尖銳批判（見05矛盾記錄）。

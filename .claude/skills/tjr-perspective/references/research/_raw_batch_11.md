## 1. 心智模型候選 (candidate mental models)

- **流動性/訂單流理論 (Liquidity & Order Flow)**：市場的每一次移動都是銀行/機構為了讓大單成交而「誘導」散戶進場/出場的結果；高點之上有 buy stop 和空頭停損，低點之下有 sell stop 和多頭停損，機構必須先「清算」這些單才能把大單塞進市場。引用：「liquidity isn't something that Banks hedge funds and institutions just like get it's something that they have to induce sweep liquidity in order for their orders to get filled」(`XxJAkUfbs0k`, 無日期，但屬於系列教學片)。同樣邏輯在 `rdz6vnXb8wE`（Boot Camp Day 10）、幾乎所有 daily bias / live trading 影片中反覆出現，是他解讀盤面的核心第一原則。

- **策略萬用於任何時間框架 (fractal/universal strategy)**：他反覆強調同一套 confluence（liquidity sweep、break of structure、fair value gap、order block、equilibrium）在週線到一分鐘線上都適用，只要「scale up 找波段、scale down 找當沖」。引用：「all of our confluences can start to come together... these concepts happen on every single time frame」(`60H1AumgjG8` Trading Transformation Day 36, 2024-05-17)；「My strategy is universal the confluences that I teach you can apply it to any time frame」(同影片)。

- **機率交易而非預測 (probability over prediction)**：交易＝長期高機率地猜對方向，靠數據(win rate + risk:reward)證明策略有效，而非追求 100% 勝率。引用：「trading is getting onto the markets every single day over a long period of time and accurately predicting where price wants to go with a high probability」(`8PYgFVB0GHE` My UPDATED Day Trading Strategy 2026, 2026-06-05)。同一影片反覆強調「64% win rate」「1 to 1.33 risk to reward」作為信心來源，而非「感覺」。

- **三大支柱：策略/風控/心理 (Strategy + Risk Management + Psychology)**：一貫框架，反覆在多支「教學型」影片出現，且明確說心理=紀律=遵守前兩者，不是玄學。引用：「There are three skill sets that we need to learn as traders... strategy, good risk management, and then your psychology... psychology literally just means sticking to both of these things」(`hBnD6T1M4w8` Path to Profitability, 2026-01-16)；同樣結構在 `qJap-CZoV6g`（6 Years of Trading Knowledge, "under 30 minutes"）重述。

- **高時間框架凌駕低時間框架 (higher timeframe holds higher power)**：當日內周期（1分鐘/5分鐘）與更高周期矛盾時，優先服從高周期偏向；只有當高周期沒有明顯 draw on liquidity 時才降頻找當沖切入。引用：「higher time frames hold higher power」(`T-Bo-9GDi3s` My Trading Strategy)；「this is a one minute break of structure... this means nothing to us because we already broke 15 minute structure」(`0ic4VBvCjsM`, 2026-04-22)。

- **雙指數互驗 (SMT divergence / ES-NASDAQ 對照)**：用 ES 與 NASDAQ 兩個指數互相印證方向；若兩者 5 分鐘趨勢不一致＝市場猶豫不決＝不交易。這是 2025-2026 期間明顯強化的模型，早期（2022-2023）影片較少提及。引用：「if ES is bearish but NASDAQ is bullish... the market is probably indecisive... I don't want to take a trade」(`8PYgFVB0GHE`, 2026-06-05)；「we had this smt divergence right here」反覆出現在多支 2025-2026 直播 (`_f15aKgX1lQ`, `mV3YFmtnRdo` 等)。

- **技能習得模型（籃球/建築工人類比）(推斷為教學隱喻，非嚴格的市場模型，但反映他對「如何學會交易」的核心信念)**：交易是一門靠重複練習累積的技能，而非靠消化更多策略影片；看影片而不下場練習＝原地踏步。引用：「it's not the tools that are the issue... it's the repetition and it's the practice」(`zZYmGcg9I_4` Trading Transformation Day 2, 教你怎麼學一項技能)。

## 2. 決策啟發式 (decision heuristics)

- 若「liquidity sweep + break of structure」在高機率位置同時出現 → 視為最佳進場點；若錯過，退而求其次找 order block 進場；再退而求其次找 fair value gap / equilibrium 進場。三層安全網結構。來源 (`T-Bo-9GDi3s` My Trading Strategy)。
- 若 ES 與 NASDAQ 5 分鐘方向不一致 → 不交易，等到兩者對齊再說。來源 (`8PYgFVB0GHE`, 2026-06-05)。
- 一天最多只想拿 1 筆交易；交易次數越少越好，因為策略的價值主要在於「阻止你進場」而非「告訴你進場」。來源（多次重複）：「your strategy is less about telling you what trade to take and more about telling you what trades not to take」(`8PYgFVB0GHE`)；「I take one trade a day and I call it good」(`339amTioakE` Boot Camp Day 17)。
- 風控：每筆交易固定風險 1–3%，或改用固定合約口數（而非每次重算 lot size），遇到高影響力新聞當天直接把倉位砍半（"de-risk"）。來源 (`hBnD6T1M4w8`；`KSiWKfPOlh4` Boot Camp Day 39)。
- 遇到 FOMC / CPI / NFP 等大新聞，預設不交易；若真的要交易新聞，則採「buy the rumor, sell the news」邏輯，等新聞前的過度擁擠倉位（如利率降息預期全部反映在價格）反轉。來源 (`egQLtQLmauk` 427k FOMC 交易, 2025-09-17)：「Market has already priced in the 25 bips rate cuts. It will cause the market to dump.」
- 進場後 TP1 一到就先平倉 50%、停損移到成本價保本；TP2 再平倉一部分、移動停損進獲利區；只留小部位吃最後的目標。此模式在幾乎每一支交易錄影/trade recap 中重複出現（如 `T-Bo-9GDi3s`, `xz7iRSBi2A0`, `YnmccW4qLng` 等）。
- 需要看到「收盤突破」而非單純的影線觸碰才算數（尤其 order block 進場：wick 只是測試，body 真正吃到才算有效反應）。來源 (`T-Bo-9GDi3s`)：「what did I say about touching the body and reacting off of it」。
- 若某天沒有給出符合策略的訊號 → 完全不交易，並將「今天沒交易」視為「贏的一天」（因為守住了紀律）。來源 (`ojErKB8wA30` Trade Recaps Are Back, 無日期)：「today was a great trading day if you ask me. I took zero trades and today was a awesome trading day because I stayed patient」。
